                                                                                    Asia Berlin Logo Exploration | Round 1   7
Concept Generation




       AsiaBerlin is a connector for startup ecosystems between Asia and Berlin.

     To start exploring a visual identity, we drew comparisons between the countries involved (by looking at
    country flags, architecture and patterns) and found visual similarities that could unite the countries, and
                                     therefore, be reflected in the logo design.




                                                                                       Asia Berlin Logo Exploration | Round 1   8
Concept Generation


    Colours

    The countries that AsiaBerlin connects to are China, Hong Kong, India, Indonesia, Japan, Singapore and Philippines.
    There will be two main colours for the AsiaBerlin brand to represent the coming together of Asia and Berlin.




                                                                                         Red is a common colour
                                                                                         amongst the country flags and
                                                                                         is a colour commonly
                                                                                         associated with Asia.
                                                                         red
                                                                                         It represents boldness and
                                                                                         energy, which are valuable
                                                                                         traits for startup companies.




                                                                                         Blue represents dependability
                                                                                         and trustworthiness, and serves
                                                                         blue
                                                                                         as a complement to the
                                                                                         boldness of the red colour.

                                                Asia Berlin Logo Exploration | Round 1   22
Concept 3



    Grid
    Within a grid, there are countless configurations that can be made by connecting different points. This alludes to the
    linking up of different startup ecosystems in Asia and Berlin, which can produce limitless possibilities.




                                                                                                Asia Berlin Logo Exploration | Round 1   23
Concept 3 | Design 4




Within a grid, there are countless configurations
that can be made by connecting different points.
This alludes to the linking up of different startup
ecosystems in Asia and Berlin, which can produce
limitless possibilities. The points joined in the logo
forms an abstract ‘AB’ for AsiaBerlin.

Any of points in the grid can be joined to create a
sub logo for the sub-brands of AsiaBerlin.



                                                         Asia Berlin Logo Exploration | Round 1   24
Concept 3 | Design 5




The idea of limitless possibilities in a grid is
further emphasized by the organic, overlapping
shapes in the logo. The points joined in the logo
forms an abstract ‘AB’ for AsiaBerlin.

Any of points in the grid can be joined to create a
sub logo for the sub-brands of AsiaBerlin.
