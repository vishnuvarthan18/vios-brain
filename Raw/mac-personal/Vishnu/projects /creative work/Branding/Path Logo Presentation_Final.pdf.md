OUR MARK

           Logomark
           The circle represents completeness and unity,
           symbolizing the holistic nature of education. Placing a
           minimalist icon inside the circle (a teacher showing a
           path to a student for success) focuses the viewer's
           attention on the central message.


           Colors
           Deep Blue represents the sense of trust, wisdom, and
           professionalism, reflecting the authority and
           knowledge of the teacher.


           Gold Symbolizes success, achievement, and
           aspiration, aligning with the goal of guiding students
           toward their bright futures.
