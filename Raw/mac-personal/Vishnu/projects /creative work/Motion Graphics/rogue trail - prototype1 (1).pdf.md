Rogue trail
                                                                                          Stage 1                                B
 Assessment: Build a Drivable Survival Vehicle Prototype
 Core Requirements (1 Week):
   1. Drivable Vehicle From A to B:
            The developer should create a vehicle that feels heavy and grounded.
            Include basic physics for the vehicle (e.g., weight, momentum, and
            terrain interaction).
            Reference Example: The sound and feel in this video
            https://www.youtube.com/watch?​v=C3p55J-​VA5k
   2. Atmosphere:
            Nighttime setting with:
                   Thick Fog: Restricts visibility and adds tension.
                   Distant Lights: Hints at locations or entities far from the vehicle.
                   Lighting Around the Vehicle: Multiple small light sources (e.g.,
                   headlights, lanterns) illuminate the immediate area, creating a
                   cozy yet vulnerable feeling.

 Optional Features (Stretch Goals):
  1. Enemies With Glowing Eyes:                                                                  A
           Place enemies (skelett1) of varying sizes that lurk around the environment.
           Their glowing eyes give a subtle indication of their presence, adding
           suspense.
           Enemies can be static or have basic wandering behavior.
  2. Freely Moving Inside the Vehicle (first person view):
           Allow the player to leave the driver’s seat and move freely around the
           vehicle while it’s stationary.
           This could involve interacting with light switches, opening windows, or
           standing in observation areas and shooting enemies.

 Keep in mind:
    This should become a multiplayer game later on
    Maps could be procedurally generated
    The game should work with a roguelike mechanic (start from zero, complete
    as many stages as possible while leveling up). Loose everything when vehicle
    is destructed (but repairs are possible)
