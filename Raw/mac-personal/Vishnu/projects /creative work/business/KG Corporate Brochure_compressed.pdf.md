KG Belief
KG’s 55-year legacy stems from being the world’s premier bearing distributor and playing a pivotal
role in making Dubai a global hub for bearing distribution. We continue to supply in this manner by
ensuring we have a strong product for each market segment: European (FAG), Japanese (NTN), and
Economical (KG). Since then, we have continued to transform to better expand our customer base,
improve our customers’ experience, and drive business resilience and agility. Some ways in which we
have adapted and transformed are:

• Modernized our core to reduce lead times, and drive organizational transparency

• Expanded our automotive product range to include spare parts for CV ﬂeet ensuring improved
  operations and ﬂeet economics

• Enhanced our industrial portfolio to include Filters, Material Handling Equipment, Gearboxes,
  Linear Motion, Automated Lubrication and Grease to cater to a more diverse range of industries

• Improved our geographic presence by appointing dedicated local resources to develop
  key markets

• Improved global visibility and service through exhibition participation, online presence, and
   frequent market visits.

KG is passionate about driving meaningful transformations for all types of customers by oﬀering
product and services. That will support growth of all our customers. We work tirelessly to ensure that
we work with our customers to solve their most challenging issues and bring cost savings wherever
possible.

We continue to grow and innovate to ﬁnd new ways to improve our customer experience and market
penetration to build on the legacy of our Founder, Kedar Gupta. Stay tuned to our various sales
channels to keep up to date on all the exciting updates we plan to launch in the coming future -
e-Commerce, an aftermarket loyalty program, and much much more!
