# Palm leaf (ollai chuvadi) visual notes
Source: viewed real photos in design/references/palm_leaf (Tirukkural_manuscript.jpg, Purananuru_manuscript.jpg,
Tamil_manuscripts_leaf.jpg, Very_Old_Tamil_manuscripts_and_pot_sherds.jpg). Values are eyeballed, not measured. 2026-09-25.
Licenses NOT yet verified per file (LICENSES.csv is empty): use as reference only.

## Single leaf (flat scan)
- Very long, thin strip. Tirukkural scan is 2081x233 = about 9:1.
- Colour: warm orange-amber, slightly darker and browner at the edges. Roughly #C98A3F centre, #A86A2C edges.
- Ends slightly uneven or rounded, with small nicks and one torn notch. Not a perfect rectangle.
- Two round holes, set IN from the ends (about 30% and 70% along), not at the corners.
- Script: dark brown-black, 5 to 7 tight lines, round letters. Text stops around each hole (a clear gap).
- Left edge often has a short column of small notes; a catalogue number in red ink may sit at one end.
- Faint horizontal grain lines run along the length.

## Bound bundle (edge view)
- Leaves stack into a dark brown block (about #4A3426) with fine horizontal lines from the stacked edges.
- Tied with orange/yellow cotton thread: vertical wraps plus X crossings, knots at the top.
- Small paper labels with numbers are tucked under the thread.
- Ends sit between plain wooden boards.

## Tools
- Stylus (ezhuthani): thin iron point in a wooden handle, with a small knife blade beside it.

## Other surfaces seen in the same museum case
- Inscribed pot sherds: reddish-brown terracotta with scratched marks.
- Inscribed conch shell, and a fan of pale bone/ivory strips.
- Photo is dim and blurry, useful as concept only, not for shipping.

## Use in design
- Leaf component: aspect 9:1, two holes at ~30% and ~70%, text wraps around holes.
- Bundle component: dark edge-on block, thread pattern, number label.
- Tone: amber and brown, never pure white or pure black.
