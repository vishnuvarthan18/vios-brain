## WEEK 1

### 1. K. (P.K.) Rajkumar, IFS — Field Director, STR

**To:** cferd@tn.nic.in
**Subject:** Introducing The Sathyamangalam Record — an independent open reference site for STR, requesting your review and guidance

Dear Thiru. K. Rajkumar, IFS,

I am writing to introduce **The Sathyamangalam Record** (sathyamangalam.online), an independent, openly licensed reference site documenting the Sathyamangalam Tiger Reserve — its geography, wildlife, tribal communities, history, and governance — built from cited primary sources including Forest Department management plans, gazette notifications, and published scientific literature.

The site is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or representing the Tamil Nadu Forest Department, the NTCA, or any government body — this is stated clearly on every page. It exists to serve as a public, well-cited, continuously-updated reference, since most existing coverage of Sathyamangalam is thin compared to its scale and significance.

The site's research is built section by section from primary sources. Its wildlife coverage alone documents 1,893 recorded species and taxa within the reserve — mammals, birds, reptiles, flora — each cross-referenced with IUCN Red List status, Wildlife Protection Act schedule, and population estimates where published data exists. This is maintained as a live, structured source database rather than a static write-up, so it can be corrected and extended as better data becomes available.

I am writing to you for a few reasons:

1. Factual accuracy — every figure on the site cites its source, but the department is the authoritative body on current data (population estimates, area figures, ranges, permit procedures). I would be grateful for any corrections you or your office are able to flag.
2. Collaboration on data and access — where publicly shareable data exists (species records, management plan excerpts, boundary data) that could strengthen the site's accuracy, I would welcome the department's guidance on what can appropriately be shared or cited.
3. Acknowledgment — if, after review, your office finds the site to be a credible and accurate independent reference, I would be glad for any acknowledgment or link you feel is appropriate — though I understand this is entirely at your discretion.
4. General support and guidance — as an independent initiative with no institutional backing, I would value any guidance on how to responsibly continue this work in a way that is useful to the department and to the public.

I am happy to walk you or your team through the site, share how it is built and sourced, or answer any questions at your convenience.

Thank you for your time and for the department's work on the reserve.

Respectfully,
Vishnuvarthan Venkatapthy
