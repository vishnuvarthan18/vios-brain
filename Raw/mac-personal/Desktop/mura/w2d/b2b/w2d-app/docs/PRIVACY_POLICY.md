Wedding2day ("W2D", "we", "us") is a business-to-business app for the wedding trade in Tamil Nadu. It is open to businesses across all 29 wedding-trade categories listed in the app — decoration, catering, photography and videos, DJ, bridal makeup, mahal or mandabam, invitation, audio and lighting, and the rest. Businesses use it to buy, sell, rent and source materials from each other, and to publish a shareable page about themselves.

This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, who can see it, and what happens when you delete your account. Please read section 3 in particular — it covers the one part of the app that publishes your contact details to people outside it.

W2D is for registered businesses and trade professionals. It is not intended for children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.


## 1. Who you are on W2D

When you sign up we collect:

- Your mobile phone number, verified with a one-time password. This is how you sign in — there is no email or password login.
- Your name
- Your business name
- Your business category — one from our fixed list of 29
- Your role — Vendor or Manufacturer. You do not type this in or choose it freely: it follows from the category you pick, and we show it to you and ask you to confirm it. It decides what you are allowed to post.
- Your district, chosen from a list of the 38 Tamil Nadu districts. This is a choice you make, not a location reading — we do not use GPS and the app never asks for location permission.
- Optionally, a GST or business-registration number if you choose to add one.

Your name, business name, category, role and district are visible to other signed-in businesses — they appear on everything you post, so the other side knows who they are dealing with. Your phone number is not: see section 4.


## 2. What you create in the app

When you post something, we store:

- The title, category, condition, price, quantity, district, delivery option and description you enter, and the date you need something by if you are posting a requirement
- Photos you choose to upload — up to 3 per post, and up to 20 on your public profile page
- Catalog entries, if you are a Manufacturer and keep a catalog

As you use the app, we also store:

- Which posts you have responded to, so you are not asked twice and so the business that posted them can see who responded
- Which businesses' phone numbers you have been shown, and which businesses have been shown yours. This is a record of two account references and a date — see section 4
- How many posts you have made, phone numbers you have revealed, and reports you have filed today. These daily counts enforce the limits described in section 4
- View and response counts on your own posts, shown to you
- Notifications sent to you inside the app, and reports you file against a post, including the reason you select
- A device token if you allow notifications, so a notification can reach this phone. It identifies the app installation, not you

## 3. Your public profile page — please read this

You can create a public profile page for your business. It is optional and you decide whether to make one.

If you create one, the following becomes visible to ANYONE who has the link — no login, no W2D account, no app needed:

- Your business name
- Your business category and district
- Your role — Vendor or Manufacturer
- Any portfolio photos you add to the page
- Your phone number, and your WhatsApp number if you add a different one — shown directly on the page, not hidden behind a tap

This is deliberate, and it is the opposite of how the rest of the app treats your number. The page exists so you can hand it to a customer who has never used W2D, and it cannot do that job if the contact details are hidden.

Three things to be aware of. First: anyone you share the link with can pass it on, and we cannot control that. Second: the page is not listed in any directory and cannot be searched or browsed — it is reachable only through its link — but it is genuinely public, so treat the link as public too. Third: your personal name is never on the page, only your business name.

Your catalog and your prices are never part of the public page. Those stay inside the app, visible only to signed-in businesses.

You can edit or remove the page at any time in Profile → Public Profile, and deleting your account removes it entirely.


## 4. Your phone number inside the app

Apart from the public profile page in section 3, your phone number is not visible to other businesses by default. It is stored separately from the rest of your profile, and another business can only see it after they choose to reveal it — one business at a time, from your post, your catalog, or a response you sent them.

Every reveal is recorded: which business was shown which number, and when. We keep those records for two reasons — so you can be shown a number you already have without it counting again, and so we can enforce a daily limit on how many numbers one account can reveal. The limit exists to stop bulk collection of contact details, and we suspend accounts that look like they are collecting rather than trading.

When you tap "Call" or "WhatsApp" after a number is revealed, the app opens your phone's dialer or WhatsApp. We have no visibility into that call or chat and keep no record of it — the conversation is between you and the other business.


## 5. Crash reports and usage statistics

We use Google Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics to see whether the app is working and being used. Being specific about this, because it is the part people most often assume is worse than it is:

- Crash reports carry your account reference and nothing else — no name, no business name, no phone number.
- The events we record ourselves carry only a post type, a business category and a district. We deliberately never log a name, a business name, a phone number or a public-profile link.
- Firebase Analytics also collects some things automatically, whichever events we log: an app-instance identifier, your device model and operating-system version, an approximate location worked out from your IP address, and session timings. We do not control that set — it comes with the tool.

## 6. Where your information is stored

Everything is stored with Google Firebase — Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics and Analytics — operated by Google. We do not run our own servers, and Google's own privacy and security practices apply to your data while it is with them.


## 7. What we don't do

- We do not sell your information to anyone.
- We do not run advertising, and there is no ad SDK in the app.
- We do not take payments, and we never collect or store card or bank details.
- There is no in-app chat or inbox on the trade side, so we hold no message history.
- We do not read your location, your contacts, your SMS messages, your calendar or your files. The app never asks for those permissions.
- The camera and photo-library permissions are used only when you choose to add a photo to a post, a catalog entry or your public profile.

## 8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete Account. We send a verification code to your registered number first, because deletion cannot be undone.

Deleting your account removes:

- Your profile — name, business name, category, role, district — and your stored phone number
- Every post you have made, and your catalog entries
- Your public profile page, so the link stops working and your contact details are no longer published
- Every photo you uploaded
- Your notifications and your notification device tokens

Three kinds of record are kept, and none of them contains your personal details once your account is gone:

- Records that you responded to another business's post. These keep that business's own counts correct, and hold only an account reference that no longer points to anything.
- Records of which businesses were shown your number and whose numbers you were shown — two account references and a date. These exist so the daily reveal limit cannot be reset by deleting and recreating an account.
- Any reports you filed, which our team needs for moderation.

If you cannot receive the verification code — for example you no longer have that number — write to us at the address in section 11 and we will delete the account for you.


## 9. Your choices

- You can edit your name, business name, category and district at any time in Profile → Edit Business Details. Changing your category may change your role, and we tell you before you save.
- You can change your registered mobile number in Profile → Change Phone Number, keeping the same account and history.
- You can create, edit or remove your public profile page at any time.
- You can turn notifications off in the app, or in your phone's system settings.
- You can block another business, and you can report a post.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top, and if the change is significant we will tell you in the app. Continuing to use W2D after a change means you accept the updated policy.
