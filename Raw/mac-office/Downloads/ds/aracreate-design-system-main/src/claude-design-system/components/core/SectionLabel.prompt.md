The araCreate section eyebrow — a number, a short dash rule, and a wide-tracked label. Marks every numbered section on the site.

```jsx
<SectionLabel number="01">araCreate Group</SectionLabel>
<SectionLabel number="02">About</SectionLabel>
<SectionLabel>Trusted by</SectionLabel>
```

Pair it above an `<h2>` to open a section. Keep labels short and in Title Case.
