The carrier of the signature edge, and the thing to reach for before inventing a component — `Panel` covers more than it looks like.

```jsx
<Panel style={{ padding: 'var(--ac-space-24)' }}>Anything.</Panel>
<Panel edge="mirror" emphasis="accent" interactive>A panel on the right of a layout.</Panel>
```

Put `surface` on the band, not on each thing inside it. `noEdge` is the opt-out — the exception is what you declare, not the rule.
