Travel and Expense Policy
Guidelines for Business Travel and Reimbursement



1. Purpose
This policy establishes guidelines for planning, approving, and reimbursing business travel and related expenses incurred by ACI employees in the course of their official duties. The policy ensures responsible use of company funds while enabling employees to travel comfortably and efficiently.
2. Scope
This policy applies to all ACI employees, field trainers, Academic Programme Managers, and contractors who incur travel and business expenses on behalf of araCreate India Private Limited. International travel guidelines apply to all India-originated travel to Germany, Sri Lanka, or other locations.
3. General Principles
All business travel must be planned in advance and pre-approved by the relevant team lead and the Managing Director for trips exceeding ₹5,000 in estimated total cost.
Employees must use the most economical and practical travel option for the purpose.
Personal comfort upgrades (e.g., upgrading from Sleeper to 3A class without prior approval) are at the employee's own expense.
All expenses must be supported by valid original receipts/invoices. Expenses without documentation will not be reimbursed.
Expense claims must be submitted within 7 working days of returning from travel.

6. International Travel – Guidelines
International travel requires MD approval at least 5 working days in advance.
Economy class for all flights. Business class requires specific MD approval and is only permitted for flights exceeding 8 hours.
Accommodation and per diem will be as per ACI-approved rates for the destination country and notified prior to travel.
Visa fees, travel insurance, and foreign exchange conversion fees are reimbursable at actuals with receipts.
Foreign currency expenses should be supported by bank statements or forex card receipts.
Per diem for Germany travel: EUR 50/day. Sri Lanka: LKR 5,000/day (equivalent).
7. Non-Reimbursable Expenses
The following expenses will not be reimbursed by ACI:
Personal entertainment, movies, or leisure activities.
Alcohol or tobacco purchases.
Traffic fines, penalties, or parking violations.
Expenses incurred by accompanying family members.
Expenses submitted without valid receipts.
Expenses incurred without prior travel authorisation.
Premium subscriptions or personal purchases made during travel.
Mobile phone bill top-ups (other than pre-approved international roaming).
8. Expense Submission Process
Complete the ACI Expense Claim Form with all details and attach original receipts.
Submit to your team lead for review and then to Finance via the designated channel.
Reimbursement will be processed in the next payroll cycle following approved submission.
Late submissions (beyond 30 days) may not be processed at ACI's discretion.
ACI may conduct spot audits of expense claims at any time.
9. Travel Advances
Travel advances may be requested for trips where estimated costs exceed ₹3,000. Advances must be requested at least 3 working days before travel through the Finance team. Unspent advance amounts must be returned to Finance within 3 working days of returning. Failure to settle advances will result in deduction from salary after due notice.
10. Policy Violations
Falsification of expense claims, submission of non-business expenses, or misuse of company funds are grounds for disciplinary action including termination and recovery of amounts through legal means.
