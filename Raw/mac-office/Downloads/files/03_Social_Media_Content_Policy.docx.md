Social Media and Content Policy
Guidelines for Online Presence and Content Creation



1. Purpose
This policy defines araCreate India Private Limited's (ACI) standards for professional and responsible use of social media and digital content creation, both in an official capacity and in personal use where ACI may be referenced. It aims to protect ACI's brand, reputation, and client relationships while enabling employees to engage positively online.
2. Scope
This policy applies to all ACI employees, contractors, interns, field trainers, and Academic Programme Managers — both for official ACI social media accounts and for personal social media accounts where ACI, its clients, partners, or students are referenced.
3. Official ACI Social Media Accounts
3.1 Authorised Management
Only personnel explicitly authorised by the Managing Director may post, comment, or respond on official ACI social media channels (LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, Twitter/X, WhatsApp Business). All official content must be reviewed and approved before publishing as per the content approval workflow.
3.2 Content Standards
All content must reflect ACI's professional brand voice — informative, credible, and community-focused.
Content must be accurate, factual, and not misleading.
Content must not disclose client names, project details, or proprietary information without written consent.
Student photos and videos from araCreate Academy sessions require explicit written consent from the institution and parent/guardian before publishing.
Content must be inclusive and must not discriminate based on gender, religion, caste, disability, or any other protected characteristic.
ACI must not comment on or share content related to political parties, political leaders, or electoral campaigns.

4. Personal Social Media Use
4.1 General Guidelines
Employees are free to use personal social media accounts during non-work hours. However, when ACI or its clients, partners, students, or projects are mentioned, the following applies:
Clearly identify any opinion as personal: 'Views are my own and not those of my employer.'
Do not share confidential, client, or project information on personal accounts.
Do not post negative, disparaging, or defamatory content about ACI, colleagues, clients, students, or partners.
Do not share photos or videos of students, client premises, or internal meetings/events without authorisation.
Do not make statements about ACI's financial performance, business plans, or legal matters.
4.2 Prohibited Personal Social Media Actions
Impersonating ACI or an ACI official in any capacity.
Creating unofficial ACI social media pages, groups, or channels.
Engaging in online harassment, trolling, or abusive behaviour, even outside working hours.
Posting content that could bring ACI into disrepute.
5. Content Creation and Intellectual Property
All content (text, design, code, video, photography, curriculum material) created by ACI employees during the course of their employment is the intellectual property of araCreate India Private Limited. Employees must not publish, share, or commercialise ACI-created content through personal channels without prior written approval from the Managing Director.
6. Brand Consistency
ACI brand assets (logo, colours, fonts, templates) must be used as per the Brand Guidelines. Any deviation requires MD approval.
The name 'araCreate' and 'araCreate Academy' must always be written correctly — lowercase 'ara', capitalised 'Create'.
The word 'programme' (not 'program') must be used in all ACI content.
7. Monitoring and Compliance
ACI reserves the right to monitor official social media channels and to periodically review content published by employees that references ACI, clients, or students in a professional context. Monitoring will be conducted in a lawful and proportionate manner.
8. Breach and Disciplinary Action
Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination. Where content published in breach of this policy has caused reputational or financial damage to ACI or its clients, legal remedies may be pursued. Employees may also be required to remove offending content immediately upon instruction.
