Child Safeguarding Policy
Protecting Children in All Our Engagements



1. Purpose
araCreate India Private Limited (ACI) is committed to creating a safe environment for all children and young persons who come into contact with our work, particularly through the araCreate Academy programmes delivered in schools, colleges, and community settings. This policy sets out the standards of behaviour expected of all ACI personnel and establishes clear procedures for responding to safeguarding concerns.
2. Scope
This policy applies to all ACI employees, contractors, interns, field trainers, academic programme managers, and any third-party personnel who work on behalf of ACI in any capacity involving direct or indirect contact with children (persons under the age of 18).
3. Definitions

4. Our Commitment
ACI recognises that:
The welfare of every child is paramount.
All children, regardless of age, gender, disability, caste, religion, or socio-economic background, have the right to protection from abuse.
ACI has a duty of care to respond to and report any concerns about a child's welfare.
This policy applies during all programme delivery, field visits, residential events, and any digital interaction with students.
5. Code of Conduct for Personnel
5.1 Required Behaviours
Maintain professional boundaries with all students at all times.
Work in visible, open spaces when interacting with students one-on-one.
Obtain written institutional permission before photographing or filming students.
Report any safeguarding concern immediately to the Designated Safeguarding Lead.
Use only ACI-approved communication channels (school/college official platforms) when communicating with students.
Treat all information about a child's welfare as strictly confidential.
5.2 Prohibited Behaviours
No ACI personnel shall be alone in a closed space with a child without a third person present.
Physical contact with children is prohibited except for safety-related assistance (e.g., first aid).
No personnel shall communicate with students via personal social media, WhatsApp, or personal phone numbers.
Sharing personal contact details with students is strictly prohibited.
No ACI personnel shall photograph, film, or record any student without explicit written consent from the institution and parent/guardian.
Using language or behaviour that could be construed as threatening, humiliating, or sexually inappropriate is strictly prohibited.
Accessing, storing, or sharing any material that sexualises children is a criminal offence and grounds for immediate termination.
6. Recruitment and Training
All personnel involved in programme delivery must undergo a background verification process prior to deployment. Field trainers and Academic Programme Managers must complete ACI's internal Child Safeguarding orientation within 30 days of joining. Refresher training is mandatory annually.
7. Recognising Signs of Abuse
ACI personnel should be alert to the following indicators of possible abuse:
Unexplained physical injuries or changes in behaviour.
A child disclosing abuse or expressing fear of a specific adult.
Signs of neglect such as poor hygiene, malnourishment, or persistent tiredness.
Sudden withdrawal from activities or peers.
Inappropriate sexual knowledge or behaviour for the child's age.
8. Reporting Procedure
8.1 Immediate Steps
Do not promise confidentiality to the child — explain that information may need to be shared to keep them safe.
Listen carefully, remain calm, and record what was said using the child's own words.
Do not investigate independently or confront any alleged abuser.
Report to the Designated Safeguarding Lead (DSL) within 2 hours of becoming aware.
8.2 DSL Responsibilities
The DSL will review the concern and determine if referral to local child welfare authorities (Childline 1098 / State Child Protection Society) is required.
The DSL will maintain a written record of all concerns, actions taken, and outcomes.
The DSL will inform the institutional contact (Principal/HOD) as appropriate.
All records are stored securely and reviewed annually.
9. Digital and Online Safety
ACI personnel must ensure that all online delivery (live sessions, recordings, LMS content) is age-appropriate and moderated. Recordings of sessions involving students shall not be shared publicly without explicit consent. Any reports of cyberbullying, grooming, or inappropriate online contact involving students linked to ACI programmes must be escalated to the DSL immediately.
10. Breach and Disciplinary Action
Any breach of this policy will be treated as a serious matter. Depending on the severity, consequences may include written warning, suspension, termination of employment or contract, and/or referral to law enforcement under the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012 and applicable provisions of the Indian Penal Code.
11. Policy Review
This policy will be reviewed annually by the Managing Director and updated as required to reflect changes in law, ACI operations, or safeguarding best practices. The next review is due: July 2027.
