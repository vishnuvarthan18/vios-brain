Smart Greeting Card Using a Simple Electrical Circuit
1. Introduction

A greeting card is traditionally used to convey wishes and messages on special occasions.
In this project, we have enhanced a handmade greeting card by incorporating a simple
electrical circuit using an LED, battery cell, and aluminium foil. When the circuit is completed,
the LED glows and gives the card an attractive illuminated effect.

2. Aim

To design and create a light-up greeting card using a simple electrical circuit made from an
LED, battery cell, and aluminium foil.

3. Materials Required

1.Greeting card / chart paper
2.LED
3.Battery cell
4.Aluminium foil
5.Adhesive tape or glue
6.Scissors
7.Colour paper and decorative materials
8.Pencil and ruler

4. Principle

The project works on the principle of an electric circuit. An LED glows when electric current
flows through it in the correct direction.

The aluminium foil acts as a conducting path between the battery and LED. When the circuit
is closed, current flows from the battery through the aluminium foil and LED and returns to
the battery, causing the LED to glow.

5. Working

1. A greeting card is prepared using paper or cardboard.

2. An LED is fixed at the desired position on the card.

3. A battery cell is placed inside or behind the card.

4. Strips of aluminium foil are arranged as conducting paths.

5. The foil connects the battery terminals to the LED terminals.
6. When the circuit is completed, current flows through the LED.

7. The LED lights up, creating an illuminated greeting card.
