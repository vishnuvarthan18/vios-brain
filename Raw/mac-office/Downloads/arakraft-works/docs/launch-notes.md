# Laser-first website redesign — 22 September 2026

The complete redesign follows the latest “araKraft Works — Website Update & Redesign Brief” and supersedes the earlier CNC product catalogue.

## Final content hierarchy

1. Supplied laser-product banner and navigation to the four priority collections.
2. Engraved photo frames: prominent manual slider with couple, family and birthday designs, thumbnail selection, keyboard/swipe support, format details and direct WhatsApp ordering.
3. Laser-engraved souvenirs: cutout stand, framed appreciation portrait and award.
4. Key tags: curated photo, date and music-design examples.
5. Restaurant menus: compact light product panel, uncropped photograph and direct WhatsApp enquiry.
6. Other laser engraving: nameplates and a custom-idea enquiry.
7. CNC: automatic gallery of six selected work images, with pause, arrow, keyboard and swipe controls. The “Discuss your idea” WhatsApp enquiry follows the gallery. No individual CNC prices or order cards.
8. Real workshop photographs in an editorial layout.
9. Five-stage process, WhatsApp/contact details, neighbourhood map and footer.

The latest final folder supplies the primary banner and selected product images. Duplicate photographs are not separate products. The menu visual uses a square image edit based on the supplied flyer, with the complete book and all edges visible. Workshop photographs come from the final folder, not synthetic product backgrounds.
