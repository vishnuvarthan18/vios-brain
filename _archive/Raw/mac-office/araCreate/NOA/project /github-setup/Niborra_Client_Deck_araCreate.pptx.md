We started from the client's formal, approved PRD (ACG-NOA-001), then grouped every requirement into 11 epics — big feature areas like “Campaign Designer” or “Fleet Management.”

Under each epic we wrote user stories — one sentence per real need, in the user's own words: “As a [user], I want [x], so that [y].” 15 stories came out of that pass, and we broke every story into the technical tasks needed to build it — 100 tasks, 306 story points in total.

Two review passes after the PRD landed added 13 more tasks it implied but didn't spell out — OTA rollback, data encryption, cross-platform testing, and more. Everything is now slotted into 10 two-week sprints running 13 Jul to 27 Nov 2026.
