          Content Creation Proposal For

    Playmobil LIFE Box


22.11.2022 | v1.0 | Aravinth Panch | BatchOne GmbH
Project
The LIFE box brings Playmobil figures with a unique
technique magically to life and immerses the player
into the fantastic world of action, adventures, fun and
educational stories.

Playmobil LIFE is an interactive toy that combines
traditional role play and modern storytelling with
digital worlds.

The scope of this proposal is to provide digital
content creation services where written stories can
become virtual games.

Requirements*
Stories
     ● 12 to 15 characters from existing Playmobil figures
     ● 2 stories per character
     ● 24 to 30 stories
     ● Minor adaptation of characters (clothing, facial expression,
        haircuts, accessories)
     ● 20 to 30 minutes of animated content per story

Assets
    ● 40 assets per story for the environment
    ● 30 assets from existing Playmobil assets
    ● 10 new assets should be developed from scratch

Requirements*
Rigging
 ● 1 rig with max 32 bones for a human character
 ● 2 to 3 side characters
 ● 1 animal character

Format
 ● Adaptation for 1024 x 600 px resolution on Android OpenGL

Timeline
 ● Deadline September 2023
 ● Product launch November 2023
