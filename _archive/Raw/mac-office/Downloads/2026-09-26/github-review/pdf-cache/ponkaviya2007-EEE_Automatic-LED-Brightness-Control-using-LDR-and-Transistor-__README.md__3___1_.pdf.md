Automatic LED Brightness Control Using LDR and Transistor

1. Aim

To design and build an automatic LED brightness control circuit using an LDR (Light
Dependent Resistor) and a transistor, where the LED brightness changes automatically
according to the surrounding light intensity.

2. Problem Statement

In many applications, lighting systems need to adjust automatically based on ambient light
conditions. Manual control is not always convenient and may lead to energy wastage.
Therefore, a simple automatic brightness control system is required that can sense light
intensity and control the LED accordingly.

3. Proposed Solution

The proposed system uses an LDR to detect ambient light levels and an NPN transistor
(BC547) to control the current flowing through the LED. The resistance of the LDR changes
with light intensity, which affects the transistor's operation and automatically adjusts the LED
brightness.

4. Components Required

   ●​   LDR (Light Dependent Resistor)
   ●​   NPN Transistor (BC547)
   ●​   LED
   ●​   Resistor
   ●​   Breadboard
   ●​   9V Battery
   ●​   Battery Connector
   ●​   Jumper Wires

5. Circuit Working

The LDR and resistor form a voltage divider connected to the base of the transistor.
In bright light, the LDR resistance decreases.
The transistor receives less base voltage, causing the LED to glow dimly or turn OFF.
In darkness, the LDR resistance increases.
The transistor receives sufficient base voltage and turns ON.
More current flows through the LED, making it glow brighter.
Thus, the LED brightness is automatically controlled according to the surrounding light
conditions.
6. Procedure

    ●​   Place the LDR, transistor, LED, and resistors on the breadboard.
    ●​   Connect the LDR and resistor as a voltage divider.
    ●​   Connect the voltage divider output to the transistor base.
    ●​   Connect the LED and current-limiting resistor to the transistor collector.
    ●​   Connect the emitter to ground.
    ●​   Connect the 9V battery to the circuit.
    ●​   Switch ON the power supply.
    ●​   Cover the LDR and observe the LED brightness increase.
    ●​   Expose the LDR to light and observe the LED brightness decrease.
