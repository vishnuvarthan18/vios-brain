Smart Fog Reduction and Highways Visibility System


Aim

To design and develop an intelligent fog reduction system that detects fog conditions on
highways and improves visibility using warm air generated by a PTC heater and blower,
while automatically controlling highway lighting through an ESP32-based monitoring system.


Problem Statement

During winter and early morning hours, dense fog forms on highways, reducing visibility for
drivers and increasing the risk of road accidents. Existing street lights only provide
illumination and cannot actively reduce fog density. Therefore, an automated system is
needed to detect fog conditions and improve visibility by reducing fog and enhancing road
lighting.


Proposed Solution

The proposed system uses DHT11 and LDR sensors to monitor temperature, humidity, and
light intensity. The ESP32 controller processes the sensor data and identifies fog conditions.
When fog is detected:
The blower and PTC heater are activated to produce warm airflow that helps disperse fog.
Highway LED lights are switched ON to improve visibility.
Real-time monitoring is provided through a Wi-Fi web dashboard.

Circuit Working

  1.​ The DHT11 sensor continuously measures temperature and humidity.
  2.​ Two LDR sensors monitor light intensity in different highway sections.
  3.​ Sensor data is sent to the ESP32 controller.
  4.​ When humidity is high and light intensity is low, the ESP32 detects fog conditions.
  5.​ Relay 1 switches ON the highway LED lights.
  6.​ Relay 2 activates the blower and PTC heater.
  7.​ The blower circulates warm air produced by the heater, helping to reduce fog
      concentration.
  8.​ All sensor values and device status are displayed on a Wi-Fi web dashboard.

Procedure

  1.​ Connect the DHT11 and LDR sensors to the ESP32.
  2.​ Connect the relay module to the ESP32 output pins.
  3.​ Connect the LED strip to Relay 1.
  4.​ Connect the blower and PTC heater to Relay 2.
  5.​ Provide 12V power supply and use a buck converter to supply 5V to the ESP32.
  6.​ Upload the program to the ESP32.
  7.​ Monitor sensor readings through the web dashboard.

       blower, and heater.

   ●​   Real-time monitoring through Wi-Fi.
   ●​   Low-cost and energy-efficient system.
   ●​   Can be installed in accident-prone areas.

Applications

   ●​   National Highways
   ●​   Expressways
   ●​   Mountain Roads
   ●​   Bridges
   ●​   Airport Runways
   ●​   Smart City Road Infrastructure
   ●​   Industrial Areas with Fog or Smoke




Result

The developed system successfully detects fog conditions using humidity and light intensity
sensors. The ESP32 automatically activates the highway LED lights, blower, and PTC
heater, resulting in improved visibility and safer driving conditions.

Conclusion

The Smart Fog Reduction and Highways Visibility System provides an effective solution for
improving road safety during foggy conditions. By combining sensor-based fog detection,
warm air circulation, automatic lighting control, and IoT monitoring, the system enhances
visibility and helps reduce accidents on highways.
