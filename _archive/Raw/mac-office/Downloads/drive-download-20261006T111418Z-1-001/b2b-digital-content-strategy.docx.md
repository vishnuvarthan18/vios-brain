araCreate Academy is the education initiative of araCreate Group, a technology company with 20+ years of real-world experience in robotics, electronics, AI and design across Germany, India and Sri Lanka.

Most schools have invested in ATL labs that sit underused, not from lack of intent, but lack of consistent, qualified delivery.

We embed directly into schools and colleges, bringing curriculum, mentors and industry exposure to a sustained weekly programme, not a one-time workshop.

This is the spine of the entire programme and should appear consistently across content.

Lab Assessment → Programme Design → Kickoff & Delivery → Progress Reporting → Student Showcase.

We are not a training vendor, we are a working technology company. Mentors are active engineers, designers and researchers, not career trainers.

Real outcomes from real students. Use sparingly and always with full context, never as a name or statistic without a story.

12 schools and 524+ students and 1,200+ youth trained in digital skills, and a validated robotics & AI pilot in Sri Lanka and India.
4.1.10 LET’S BUILD THIS TOGETHER
A clear call to action — lab visit, proposal, or conversation starter. Every piece of B2B content should end with a path to act.

VCET Partnership — Content Brief (Social Media Series)
Context & background
VCET trains EEE (Electrical & Electronics Engineering) and ECE (Electronics & Communication Engineering) students with one clear goal: get them placed in electronics companies. Their gap is the standard one — a strong academic syllabus that doesn't by itself make a graduate "industry-ready." Companies hiring EEE/ECE engineers want hands-on, applied skill, not just marks.
That's the exact gap araCreate is brought in to close, embedded inside VCET rather than as an outside vendor.
Two things make this partnership worth telling as a story rather than a one-line credential:
It starts early. Students are brought in from second year itself, not as a final-year, pre-placement crash course. That's 2+ extra years of runway before placement season.
It's recurring, not a one-off. VCET plans this every year — it's a standing part of their calendar, not a single workshop or guest lecture.
The story arc
The training runs as a visible progression, not a single skill: it starts with something as small as making an LED blink, then moves through guided projects, into the students' own ideas and problem statements, and ends at product engineering and exposure to real industrial process.
