Quick reference:
- **Colors:** Golden Sun `#F9BF3B` (accent), Graphite Gray `#555555` (text), Black `#222222`, Stroke `#CECECE`, Canvas `#F6F6F6`. Stay in palette.
- **Type:** Monument Extended (logo wordmark ONLY) + Poppins (all headings, titles, body & UI — lean on Light 300, Bold 700 for metrics) + Red Hat Mono (specs). Eyebrows & buttons are wide-tracked UPPERCASE.
- **Shape:** buttons square, cards 20px radius, hairline borders, low neutral shadows, hover lifts ~5px with soft shadow. No emoji. Imagery is isometric illustration + duotint photos.
- **Voice:** professional, clear, friendly. "We" / "you". Tagline: *"Empowering ideas from mind to market."* 360° services.
- **Tokens:** link `styles.css`. **Components:** `_ds_bundle.js` → `(secret removed)`. **Assets:** `assets/`.
