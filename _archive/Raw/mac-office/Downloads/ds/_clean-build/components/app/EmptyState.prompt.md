```jsx
<EmptyState art="assets/illustrations/charts-pie-and-bars.svg"
  title="No jobs yet"
  text="A job is one piece of work for one client. Create the first one and it appears here."
  action={<Button>New job</Button>} />
```

Three kinds and they read differently: **nothing yet** (say what the thing is and offer to make one), **nothing found** (offer to clear the filters), **nothing wrong but nothing here** (say when it will fill). Never "No data" — that tells the visitor what you do not have rather than what they can do.
