```jsx
  <AccordionItem title="Do I need any experience?" open>
    <p>No. The introductory courses assume nothing.</p>
  </AccordionItem>
  <AccordionItem title="What if I miss a session?">
    <p>Every session is recorded and stays up for the length of the course.</p>
  </AccordionItem>
</Accordion>
```

**Do not hide anything important in an accordion** — collapsed content is not read, is weaker for search, and is missed by anyone skimming. Prices, delivery times and warnings belong on the page. Default to letting several open at once; people compare answers.
