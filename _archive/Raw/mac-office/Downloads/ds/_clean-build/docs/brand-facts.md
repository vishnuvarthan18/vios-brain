# BRAND FACTS

Every fact about araCreate that copy, a slide, a mock-up or a demo page is
allowed to state. **Nothing on this page may be invented, rounded, updated or
improved.** If a number you need is not here, the answer is to ask Vishnu, not
to estimate.

This exists because design systems generate placeholder copy, and placeholder
copy has a habit of shipping. A wrong client count in a starter kit becomes a
wrong client count in a pitch deck. So: these strings, verbatim, or none.

Source: araCreate Brand Guidelines v1.0 (2026) and the araCreate Deck of
4 February 2026, by way of the team’s earlier design system (ACDS). Recorded
here 20 August 2026.

## How the name is written

- **araCreate** — lowercase `a`, lowercase `r`, lowercase `a`, capital `C`. Not
  "AraCreate", not "Aracreate", not "ARACreate".
- **ARACREATE** in full capitals is correct **only** as the logo wordmark set in
  Monument Extended. It is never body copy, never a heading, never a page title.
- **araCreate Group** is the parent. A single company is `araCreate GmbH`.

## What is not a fact

These are absent on purpose. If a design needs one, it is a question for
Vishnu, not a gap to fill:

- Revenue, headcount by office, or any growth figure
- Named clients, beyond what a signed testimonial already says in public
- Named team members other than the founder
- Certifications, awards or memberships
- Any date other than the 2003 founding
- Anything about pricing

Where a component genuinely needs filler, use obvious filler — `Client name`,
`00+`, `Lorem ipsum`. A visible placeholder gets replaced. A plausible
invention does not.
