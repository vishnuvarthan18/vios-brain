   How to actually use this

   Don't spend six months or any money chasing "blockchain investigator" as a job title in
   India — the postings barely exist, and the ones that do want 1–3 years' experience
   plus SQL and Python. Instead: learn the free tools this month (a weekend, not a
   course), reproduce two or three publicly documented crypto-crime cases from
   Rekt.news as portfolio pieces, and use that as a talking point inside an AML/fraud
   analyst application — which is a real, high-volume job (see the first guide). Crypto
   becomes your specialisation inside that job in year two, once you have SQL and
   Python and can credibly apply for the vendor-side investigator roles that actually
   require them.

DOOR THREE

OSINT and crime-tech: the
one to actually build toward
This is where your instinct was right, just aimed slightly wide. Not "AI jobs" —
investigation and analysis jobs that increasingly run on digital tools, at
companies that already sell to Indian police and corporate clients.



  1      OSINT Analyst, Innefu Labs           VERIFIED · FRESHER-ELIGIBLE

         Delhi · 0–1 years experience

         A genuinely verified posting from an Indian AI/OSINT firm that sells to law
         enforcement. Asked for social-media fluency and communication skill, not an
         engineering degree. This is the single clearest match for your exact profile found
         anywhere in this research — watch their careers page and set a LinkedIn alert.



  2      Business Intelligence Analyst, Kroll           REAL, LIVE POSTING

         Mumbai · Analyst I tier

         Kroll's Business Intelligence and Investigations practice does corporate due
         diligence and integrity research — reading public filings, litigation records, and
         media to build a picture of a person or company. This is investigative research
         work, and it is exactly what a criminology degree trains you to do. Pinkerton runs
         similar Intelligence Analyst postings globally, sometimes open to India.



  3      I4C Analyst — 195-post recruitment
          CONTRACT, 3-YEAR, WATCH FOR NEXT CYCLE

         ₹65,000/month (~₹7.8 LPA) · Ministry of Home Affairs

         This actually happened in May 2026 — 195 contractual posts at India's Cybercrime
         Coordination Centre, including 57 Analyst-tier roles at ₹65,000/month, alongside
         Consultant and Senior Consultant posts. It's contractual, not permanent, and the
         cycle has closed — but it confirms MHA runs real, paid, periodic analyst-tier
         recruitment. Register for job alerts on i4c.mha.gov.in and check back.



  4      SOC Analyst, fresher tier         HIGHEST VOLUME, WEAKEST FIT

         ₹2.5–4.5 LPA · Sequretek and similar firms run explicit fresher postings

         The most plentiful technical-adjacent entry point in India, and Sequretek has a
         verified "SOC Analyst – Fresher" role on its own careers portal. Honest caveat: this
         is fundamentally a cybersecurity monitoring job — logs, alerts, SIEM tools — not a
         criminology-flavoured role. It needs basic networking fundamentals, which the
         roadmap below covers, but don't expect your degree to matter much here. Use it as
         a fallback, not a first choice.



  5      Cyber Threat Intelligence Analyst, CloudSEK
     SMALL BUT REAL, INTERNSHIP FUNNEL EXISTS

    Bengaluru · verified internship-to-hire posting on Greenhouse

    CloudSEK does dark-web monitoring and digital risk protection and has a
    documented internship pipeline. The market for pure threat-intel analyst roles in
    India is small — dozens, not hundreds, of genuinely fresher-open postings — but it
    exists and is criminology-adjacent (reading forums, tracking threat actors, writing
    intelligence reports).



✕   Digital forensics practitioner           CLOSED WITHOUT A BRIDGE CREDENTIAL


    The honest finding: this field is gatekept by CS/IT/forensic-science degrees and
    expensive certifications (CHFI runs well over ₹25,000). A criminology-only graduate
    is not a natural fit for the core technical forensic work in India — you'd need an
    additional forensic-science or CS bridge qualification first. Rule this out as a near-
    term target.



✕   "Crime Analyst" as an Indian job title            DOESN'T REALLY EXIST HERE


    In the US and UK, police departments directly employ civilian crime analysts for
    hotspot mapping and pattern analysis. In India, this function barely exists as a
    standing civilian role — it's absorbed into police-tech vendor "business analyst"
    posts or NGO research roles instead. Naukri's "793 crime analyst jobs" figure is
    dominated by US/UK remote postings and financial-crime analyst roles wrongly
    tagged. Don't search this exact title.



✕   CCTNS / NATGRID / ICJS contractor roles                EFFECTIVELY ENGINEERING-ONLY


    These national criminal-justice IT systems are built and staffed by Wipro, TCS, C-
    DAC, and NIC almost entirely through software-engineering and systems-
    integration hiring. No domain-analyst hiring pipeline was found. Not worth six
    months of study aimed here.



✕   MHA "Cyber Crime Volunteer" programme
     NOT A JOB — UNPAID, AND CONTROVERSIAL


    This is real, but it is an unpaid volunteer scheme for flagging content, not
    employment, and it drew significant civil-liberties criticism including a PIB
    clarification. Do not plan around it as income.
CORRECTIONS

Five things the hype gets wrong


  THE CLAIM

  TRM Labs and Chainalysis certifications are free, and getting one leads straight to a
  crypto-investigator job.
  FALSE

  TRM's certifications cost $1,000–1,100 each. Chainalysis restructured in January 2026 so
  its real certifications come bundled with a paid software subscription — issued to people
  already employed by a client, not to job-seekers. Neither firm has an India investigator
  opening right now.


  THE CLAIM

  Merkle Science, based in Bengaluru, is hiring blockchain investigators in India.
  FALSE

  Merkle Science's own careers page lists New York as headquarters. Its Bangalore
  postings are Software Engineer Intern, Director of Finance, and Marketing Operations —
  zero investigator or analyst roles.


  THE CLAIM

  AI companies are actively recruiting criminology graduates for red-teaming because of the
  harm-category overlap.
  UNSUPPORTED

  The overlap is conceptually real — CSAM, terrorism, and trafficking are genuine red-
  teaming categories — but no job description found anywhere named criminology, criminal
  justice, or law enforcement as a preferred qualification. You'll compete on demonstrated
  skill, not the credential.


  THE CLAIM

  Remote AI red-teaming gig work (Outlier, Mercor) pays $15–75/hr reliably.
  PARTLY TRUE, IMPORTANTLY INCOMPLETE

  The pay rates are real when work is available. Outlier's own structure means "some
  weeks 40 hours, some weeks zero" — it is unstable by design. Treat it as supplemental
  income during study, never as your primary income plan.


  THE CLAIM

  "Crime Analyst" is a standing civilian job in Indian policing, like in the US or UK.
  FALSE
     This job title barely exists as an Indian civilian role. Search results under this exact term
     are dominated by US/UK remote postings and mislabelled financial-crime analyst jobs.
     Search for OSINT Analyst, Business Intelligence Analyst, or Threat Intelligence Analyst
     instead — those are the real Indian equivalents.

CONSOLIDATE AND PUSH

   Revisit weak SQL and Python areas with more practice problems.

   Build one portfolio site linking all four deliverables — a cleaned dataset, two dashboards, a
   home-lab writeup, an OSINT case study, and a policy analysis.

   Mock interviews. Tailor a resume and cover letter for each of the three tracks — OSINT/BI
   analyst, AML/fraud analyst (from the first guide), and SOC analyst as a fallback.

   Apply hard, across all three tracks simultaneously, not sequentially.



   The budget

   Google Data Analytics Certificate, one fast month (~₹3,000) · TryHackMe Premium,
   two months (~₹2,000–3,000) · an optional OSINT paid course if the free material feels
   thin (~₹2,500–3,500) · an optional SWAYAM certificate exam fee (~₹1,200) · a small
   buffer. Total: roughly ₹12,000–14,700. Everything else in this plan — Excel, SQL,
   Python basics, Power BI, Tableau, Bellingcat's OSINT toolkit, Trace Labs, the legal
   codes — is free. Skip PL-300 (₹14,000+ alone), Security+ (₹34,000+), and GIAC's
   GOSI ($999) entirely; none of them are necessary at fresher level, and each alone
   would blow the budget.
