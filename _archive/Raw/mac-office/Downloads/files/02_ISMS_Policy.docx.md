Information Security Management System (ISMS) Policy
Aligned with ISO/IEC 27001:2022



1. Purpose
This Information Security Management System (ISMS) Policy establishes the framework for protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information assets held or processed by araCreate India Private Limited (ACI). This policy demonstrates ACI's commitment to implementing and continually improving information security in alignment with ISO/IEC 27001:2022.
2. Scope
This policy applies to all information assets, systems, networks, and data processing activities of ACI — including those in Erode (India HQ), Germany, and Sri Lanka operations — and to all employees, contractors, interns, third-party vendors, and any person authorised to access ACI information systems.
3. Information Security Objectives
Protect client, partner, and employee data from unauthorised access, disclosure, or modification.
Ensure business continuity through risk management and incident response preparedness.
Meet legal, regulatory, and contractual obligations related to data protection.
Promote a culture of information security awareness across all ACI operations.
Achieve and maintain ISO 27001 certification as a demonstration of commitment to clients.
4. Information Classification

5. Access Control
Access to ACI information systems and data must be granted on a least-privilege, need-to-know basis. All access rights must be formally requested, approved by the relevant team lead or the Managing Director, and reviewed quarterly. Departing employees must have all access revoked within 24 hours of their last working day.
All systems must be protected by strong, unique passwords (minimum 12 characters, alphanumeric with symbols).
Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all cloud systems, email accounts, and remote access.
Shared credentials are prohibited. Every individual must have a unique user account.
Third-party access must be governed by a Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) and time-bound access grants.
6. Acceptable Use of Information Assets
ACI systems and equipment must be used for business purposes only.
Employees must not install unauthorised software on ACI-owned or ACI-managed devices.
Removable media (USB drives, external hard disks) must be encrypted before use with sensitive data.
Company data must not be stored on personal devices or personal cloud accounts (e.g., personal Google Drive, Dropbox) unless explicitly approved.
Sensitive information must not be transmitted via unencrypted channels (e.g., plain email for Restricted data).
7. Physical and Environmental Security
Physical access to ACI office premises and server areas is restricted to authorised personnel.
Clear desk and clear screen policies must be followed — lock screens when stepping away.
Printed documents containing Confidential or Restricted data must be shredded after use.
Visitors must be logged, escorted, and never left unattended in work areas.
8. Network and System Security
ACI networks must be protected by firewalls, and all traffic must be monitored.
Wireless networks must use WPA3 encryption and separate guest networks must be used for visitors.
All software and operating systems must be kept up to date with security patches within 30 days of release.
Anti-malware software must be installed and active on all endpoint devices.
Remote access to ACI systems must use a VPN with MFA.
9. Data Protection and Privacy
ACI processes personal data of employees, clients, students, and partners. All such data must be handled in compliance with applicable data protection laws including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India) and GDPR requirements for the Germany entity. Personal data must not be retained beyond its stated purpose and must be securely deleted thereafter.
10. Incident Management
10.1 Reporting
All information security incidents (suspected breaches, malware, unauthorised access, lost devices) must be reported to the Information Security Designated Contact within 1 hour of discovery. Personnel must not attempt to investigate or resolve incidents independently.
10.2 Response
Incidents will be logged, classified by severity, and investigated within 24 hours.
Clients and affected parties will be notified in line with legal obligations (within 72 hours for GDPR-applicable incidents).
Post-incident reviews will be conducted for all Severity 1 and 2 incidents.
11. Business Continuity and Backup
Critical business data must be backed up daily to an encrypted, offsite or cloud location.
Backups must be tested for restorability at least once every quarter.
A Business Continuity Plan (BCP) covering key services must be documented and tested annually.
12. Supplier and Third-Party Security
All third-party vendors with access to ACI data or systems must sign an NDA and a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) before onboarding. Vendor security practices must be reviewed annually. ACI reserves the right to audit vendor compliance with this policy.
13. Training and Awareness
All ACI personnel must complete information security awareness training within 30 days of joining and annually thereafter. Phishing simulation exercises may be conducted periodically to assess awareness levels.
14. Compliance and Audit
ACI will conduct internal ISMS audits at least once per year. Any non-conformities identified must be remediated within agreed timelines. The ISMS Policy and associated controls will be reviewed annually or after any significant incident or organisational change.
15. Consequences of Non-Compliance
Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment or contract. Deliberate or negligent breaches that result in data loss or reputational harm may also be subject to legal action under applicable Indian and international law.
