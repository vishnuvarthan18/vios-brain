How the teaching adapted
  Start simple
  Day 1 was an LED on a paper greeting card. Each day then added
  one new idea to what they had built.




  Demo, then challenge
  Attention held for about 15 minutes. So we gave a short demo,
  then a task to make something work.




  One part, many uses
  "Change one box, get a new project." The same sensor and servo
  came back in parking and flood warnings.
