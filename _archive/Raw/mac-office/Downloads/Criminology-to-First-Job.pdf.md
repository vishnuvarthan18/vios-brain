Criminology to First Job
You asked for jobs where a criminology degree is the named
qualification. I went looking for all of them. There are six — and one

And those 30 are the decisive part. I pulled the full list. Every single one is senior —
Senior Manager Financial Crime Compliance (15–20 years), Director Financial Crime

Hyderabad (7–9 years). In each, "criminology" appears only as a stray word inside a

Naukri advertises "6,351 criminology jobs in India." That number is keyword matching
against any JD containing crime, criminal, investigation, forensic. LinkedIn's exact-
phrase search returns 7. Ignore the big numbers on every board — they will waste
weeks of your life.



   The reframe that makes this solvable

   Almost nothing in India requires criminology. A very large number of roles accept it
   under the words "Any Graduate" — and in the crime-adjacent subset of those, you are
   a genuinely stronger candidate than the B.Com applying beside you. Your degree's
   value is realised at the interview, not at the filter. So: stop searching for the degree
   name. Start searching for the function. Then use the degree to win the room.

   What this list tells you to do

   Two things. Tamil Nadu is the only state whose service rules write your degree
   into bachelor's-level eligibility — everywhere else that names criminology (Delhi,
   Telangana) names it at master's level only. And the entries that pay properly are all
   master's-gated. That is not a reason to despair; it is a reason to treat the master's as
   a scheduled move rather than a vague someday. Section five prices it.

THE ONE PARAGRAPH THAT WINS THE INTERVIEW



       “Most people on an AML floor learned what a red flag is from a training deck. I spent
       three years studying why people commit crime and how an investigation is built and
       evidenced. I read a transaction pattern as behaviour, not just as an alert.”


Then back it: predicate offences, proceeds of crime, how organised crime moves value,
layering and integration, why a suspicious-activity narrative has to survive a prosecutor
reading it. Almost no B.Com fresher can hold that conversation. Read India's FATF mutual
evaluation report before the interview — it is free, and nobody at your level has read it.

   Sequence this by your age, today

   If you are 21–23: attack SSC CPO and UPSC CAPF AC before a master's. A two-
   year degree will eat the entire 25-year eligibility window, and those are the two best
   officer-grade doors open to any graduate. Prepare while working the AML job.

   If you are 24 or over: the 25-cap exams are effectively gone. Aim at SSC CGL (30),
   IB ACIO (27), RPF SI (28) and UPSC CSE (32) — and because those are longer
   games, the master's becomes the higher-priority move, not the competing one.




THE DEGREE UPGRADE

What to study next, ranked
by employability per rupee
Every well-paid criminology-named post in India requires a master's. Rather
than treat that as bad news, price the options. The winner is not the one you
would expect.



   1      3-year LLB at a government university                   BEST VALUE IN INDIA

          ₹15,000–60,000 total · 3 years · CUET-PG (DU, BHU) or MH CET Law · no
          upper age limit · any discipline

          Roughly the cost of one month's rent, for three years. Delhi University alone has
          2,888 seats across its three law centres. It converts you from "criminology
          graduate, discipline not listed" into "advocate" — and advocate is the qualification
          that Project 39A, Vidhi, CHRI, HRLN, iProbono and the whole legal-aid system
          actually hire on. Vidhi research fellows earn ₹8–10 LPA. Criminology plus law is the
          strongest combination available to you, and it is nearly free. The cost is three years
          of forgone income.
2   MSW — Master of Social Work               FIXES THE NAMING PROBLEM

    ₹20,000–1,60,000 · 2 years · CUET-PG or institutional

    Unglamorous and the pragmatic winner. MSW appears on every DCPU, One Stop
    Centre, Juvenile Justice Board, Child Welfare Committee and de-addiction-centre
    eligibility list — the exact lists that currently omit criminology and screen you out. It
    also matters abroad: Social Worker (ANZSCO 272511) is on Australia's Core
    Skills Occupations List; criminologist is not, and nor is intelligence analyst,
    fraud analyst or security consultant. Immediately employable at ₹18,000–
    28,000/month in scheme-funded posts.



3   MA Social Work (Criminology & Justice), TISS Mumbai
     BOTH PROBLEMS AT ONCE

    ₹1,63,900 total · 2 years · CUET-PG · intake 30

    Exceptional value and the best specialist option in the country: it is an MSW and a
    criminology qualification simultaneously, with fieldwork placed inside prisons,
    courts, police stations and protective homes through the Prayas project, which
    TISS has run since 1990. The catch is an intake of thirty, so it is fiercely
    competitive. Apply, and have a backup. Note that TISS PG admission now runs
    through CUET-PG, not the old TISSNET — coaching material has not caught up.
    Fee figures on aggregator sites are wildly wrong (one says ₹9 lakh, another
    ₹34,000); call 022-25525252.



4   MA Criminology, LNJN NICFS Delhi                 NO ENTRANCE EXAM

    2 years · merit on your last qualifying exam · bachelor's in criminology,
    50%

    India's national criminology institute, under the Ministry of Home Affairs, degree
    awarded through GGSIPU. Merit-based with no entrance test — a genuine
    safety net, and criminology is explicitly the preferred entry degree, which is true
    almost nowhere else. What it unlocks is concrete: the DSSSB Delhi block of 80
    Welfare Officer / Probation Officer / Prison Welfare Officer posts at Level 6
    (₹35,400–1,12,400), FSL Delhi's Junior Scientific Assistant (Lie Detection) at Level
    5, Telangana's District Probation Officer, the BPR&D and NHRC internships, and
    UGC-NET Criminology.



5   LNJN NICFS one-year PG Diplomas                  UNDERRATED · FAST

    1 year · merit-based, no entrance exam

    Three of them: Investigation of Cyber Crime & Law, Security Management, and
    Victimology & Victim Assistance. A fast, cheap, Government-of-India-brand
    credential that pairs unusually well with the private-sector fraud and security route
    — you can hold the job and the diploma in the same year.



6   MSc / MA Criminology at a state university              CHEAP AND CREDIBLE

    Univ of Madras ₹80,987 total · Lucknow University ₹60,320 total · NFSU
    ₹60,000/semester

    University of Madras runs India's oldest criminology department and hosts the
    Indian Society of Criminology; Lucknow's MA Criminology & Forensic Science is
    the cheapest credible option and accepts any bachelor's at 45%. NFSU
    Gandhinagar's MA Criminology (Forensic Psychology) has mandatory internships
    with police departments, FSLs, prisons and human rights commissions — a real
    advantage — but note it takes any bachelor's at 55%, so your criminology degree
    earns no formal preference there. Careful with NFSU's headline "₹6 LPA average,
    90–95% placement" figures: those are its MBA programmes, not criminology.



—   MSc Forensic Science           PROBABLY INELIGIBLE


    NFSU requires a bachelor's in "Science / Forensic Science / Medicine /
    Engineering / Pharmacy / Dentistry / Ayush" at 55%. A BA Criminology is
    ineligible. A BSc Criminology is arguable but not guaranteed — it depends on
    whether your awarding university's BSc counts as a science degree. Email
    admissions@nfsu.ac.in and get the answer in writing before you spend a rupee
    preparing for it.



—   MA Psychology · MBA in risk · overseas master's now                   NOT YET


    MA Psychology only pays off if you commit to the full RCI-recognised M.Phil/PsyD
    clinical route — four-plus years, and a plain MA is not a licence to practise. An MBA
    at a tier-3 school buys a criminology graduate very little. And going abroad now is
    the expensive mistake: a UK criminology master's costs ₹35–48 lakh all-in, the
    Graduate Route drops to 18 months for anyone applying from 1 January 2027, UK
    police forces require indefinite leave to remain, prison and probation posts are Civil
    Service roles with the same bar — and HESA's own data shows UK criminology
    graduates' most common destination fifteen months out is retail and customer
    service (19.9%), earning below the all-graduate average. Revisit abroad after a
    master's or LLB plus two years' experience, when the Gulf becomes achievable on
    merit.

CORRECTIONS

Nine things you will
be told that aren't true
Searches on this topic return overwhelmingly education-marketing content. I
checked the most-repeated claims against notifications, gazette rules and
awarding bodies. These are the ones that fail.


  THE CLAIM

  Criminology graduates are eligible for state Forensic Science Laboratory Scientific
  Assistant posts.
  FALSE

  FSL science posts require Chemistry, Physics, Biology, Forensic Science or Computer
  Science. Maharashtra's 2024 recruitment of 125 FSL posts names criminology nowhere.
  The only exception is master's-level lie-detection and psychology streams — FSL Delhi's
  Junior Scientific Assistant (Lie Detection) requires a Master's in Psychology or
  Criminology, by gazette rule.


  THE CLAIM
Criminology graduates get preference, bonus marks or a quota in police SI and constable
recruitment.
FALSE

No such clause exists in Kerala or Tamil Nadu — the two states most often cited. Both are
plainly "any bachelor's degree". Treat any version of this claim as false unless it quotes a
notification clause.


THE CLAIM

Anti Human Trafficking Units recruit criminology graduates.
UNSUPPORTED

No civilian AHTU recruitment notification exists in any state. AHTUs are staffed by serving
police personnel on deputation within the police hierarchy. There is no graduate entry
route.


THE CLAIM

DCPU and Mission Vatsalya posts commonly name criminology.
FALSE

Mission Vatsalya's own guidelines never mention criminology, and the scheme's indicative
qualifications for all ten DCPU positions key to Social Work, Sociology, Child
Development, Law and Statistics. Delhi's and Tamil Nadu's district notifications confirm it.
Apply anyway — but this is why the MSW matters.


THE CLAIM

CBI, IB and NIA recruit criminology graduates directly.
MISLEADING

They recruit through SSC CGL and UPSC, which are open to any graduate, or by
deputation from the police. NIA runs no general graduate exam; its own advertisements
are deputation or support-cadre. The route to CBI Sub-Inspector is SSC CGL, full stop.


THE CLAIM

Top recruiters of Indian criminology graduates include UNDP, CBI and the FBI, at ₹3–10
LPA rising to ₹30 LPA.
FALSE

This appears on Collegedunia with no sources cited. The FBI does not recruit Indian
criminology graduates. Compare it against what actually happened this year: the one
government post that explicitly named criminology paid ₹15,000 a month, and wanted a
master's.


THE CLAIM

Private detective work is a natural career for criminology graduates.
    RECOMMEND AGAINST

    The sector is entirely unregulated — no licence, no registration, and the 2007 regulation
    bill lapsed. In Himanshu Chordia v. State of Rajasthan (Supreme Court, 31 July 2026) the
    Court flagged private-agency evidence gathering for admissibility failures under s.65B,
    privacy invasion, and liability under IPC s.354C (voyeurism). Poor pay, real personal legal
    exposure, no exit credential. Matrimonial surveillance is most of the actual revenue.


    THE CLAIM

    You need two years' experience to sit the CFE exam.
    FALSE

    ACFE's own eligibility page: 40 points to sit, 50 points plus two years to be certified. A
    bachelor's is 40 points, no field of study specified. You can sit it now. Indian training
    providers saying otherwise are contradicting the awarding body.


    THE CLAIM

    Naukri has 6,351 criminology jobs and LinkedIn has 3,000+.
    KEYWORD NOISE

    Both are loose matches against any JD containing crime, criminal, investigation or
    forensic. LinkedIn's exact-phrase count is 7. foundit's real count is 30, all senior. On
    foundit, "criminology" surfaces an Application Developer at Barclays and a Director of QA
    at Amex.

How this was researched, and where it is thin. Everything here comes from official
recruitment notifications, gazette recruitment rules, state PSC sites, awarding bodies' own
eligibility pages, live job postings, or salary aggregators with stated sample sizes. Where a
claim rests only on a secondary source, I have said so in the text. Searches on this topic
return overwhelmingly education-marketing content, and where I checked those pages
directly they contained no employer names, no salary methodology and no labour-market
data — so none of them are load-bearing here.

Verify before you spend money or miss a deadline. TNPSC's own site and several
state and ministry domains were unreachable during this research, so a few items rest on
two independent sources quoting identical notification wording rather than the PDF itself
— the TNPSC DCPO qualification is one. Fees, visa rules and exam dates change. The
two things I would re-check first: SSC CPO 2026's current status on ssc.gov.in, and
whether your BSc counts as a science degree for NFSU, in writing, before planning
around forensic science.

Two things would sharpen this considerably: your age, because it decides whether the
25-cap exams come before or after a master's; and whether you read and write Tamil,
because it decides whether Tamil Nadu — the only state that names your degree at
bachelor's level — is a real option or a closed one.
