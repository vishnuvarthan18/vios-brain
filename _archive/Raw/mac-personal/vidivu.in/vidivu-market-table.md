## Where Vidivu actually fits

**The crowded lane:** every player above competes on either tenure (18-21 years) or scale/funding signals. Vidivu has neither and shouldn't pretend to — that contest is unwinnable and fabricating it is a real legal and reputational risk.

**Three genuine openings:**

1. **The SME modernization buyer is underserved.** Nearly every competitor speaks in startup/"build me an app" language. The owner of an existing business with spreadsheets, a legacy system, and no technical staff has almost nowhere in this market that talks to them plainly. Vidivu's `/smes` page targets exactly this.

2. **Tier-2 India is an open field.** Digital adoption there is growing ~40% faster than metros, with 65%+ of Indian SMEs raising digital spend and government subsidies covering 30-50% of adoption cost. Every competitor above optimizes for "Chennai" — nobody is contesting Coimbatore, Madurai, Surat, or Rajkot.

3. **Transparency as differentiation.** Code ownership, NDA on every project, milestone billing, and direct founder access are all things Vidivu can state truthfully today. F22 Labs proves the founder-visibility play works at this scale.

**What can't be shortcut:** every company in this table has years of backlinks, review-platform presence, and published case studies. On-page SEO closes none of that gap. The single highest-leverage move is shipping real work and publishing it as case studies with actual metrics — Tentosoft's page shows exactly how much that format outperforms generic capability claims.
