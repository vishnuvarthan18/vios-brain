# T5d - Physics: sherd and stone

Read `design/realism/AGENT_BRIEF.md` first (rules, tools, start/finish steps).

Sherd: rigid irregular piece (convex-ish outline, thickness from spec estimate) dropped on a table: falls, settles, no
penetration, determinism. Stone: "zero movement under any input" (already a test; extend to the site-scale slab with
pointer drag, keyboard and resize inputs). Known-bad variants. Demo `design/realism/t5/sherd_stone.html`. Commit.
