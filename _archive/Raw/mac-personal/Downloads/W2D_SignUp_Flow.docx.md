Wedding2day — Sign-Up & First-Use Flow
Please read through and tell us: is this what actually happens on your phone? Mark anything that's wrong, missing, or confusing.
1. If you are a REQUESTER (Vendor)
Examples: Decorator, Caterer, DJ, Photographer, Bridal Makeup, Priest, Musicians, Event Host, Venue Owner, Marriage Counsellor, Travel/Finance services — you need materials or products for a wedding.
2. If you are a SUPPLIER (Manufacturer)
Examples: Decoration material makers, garland makers, furniture, invitations, return gifts, ornament rental, stage/lighting/LED wall — you make or supply physical products.
Note: every business picks ONE type only — you cannot be both a Requester and a Supplier on the same account.
