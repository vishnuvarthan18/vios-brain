OUR STORY
Digital Supply Chain Platform Limited is one of the leading technology and software automation
companies serving clients across East Africa in the Distribution Sector. We are pioneers in the integration
of technology in distribution value chains, enabling our customers to deliver exceptional value and
maximize on profits. We are trusted partners in the journey of digital transformation for FMCGs.

Our story began in 2020. The COVID -19 global pandemic paralyzed global supply chain systems in
unprecedented ways. Factory shutdowns and disruptions in flow of goods exposed a dire need for
significant transformation in distribution. Traditional methods would no longer suffice in the new normal.

We saw the need and seized the opportunity to build supply chain systems that are resilient, provide
real-time insights and foster collaboration within the whole value chain.

Digital Supply Chain Platform Limited was incorporated to create various digital platforms to meet these
core objectives.

We are your preferred partner in your digital transformation journey.

PRODUCT OVERVIEW
Our flagship product is the Digital Distribution Solutions (DDS). It is an
innovation solely developed to address the specific challenges faced by
FMCGs in distribution.
Many companies still rely on manual processes that are inefficient and
expensive. Many companies have automated the payment process only,
thereby leaving a large part of the process grossly inefficient.
DDS is a cloud enabled platform that is focused on the end to end
automation and digitization of the distribution in supply chain companies.
These processes include:
•   Supplier & Customer Management
•   Ordering
•   Invoicing & Journey Management
•   Real time Payment Collections
Whilst integrating to relevant third parties at different data points.
