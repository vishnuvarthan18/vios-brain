                                                                                                                         5
LOGO USAGE




Outsell’s logo is used primarily on a white background. Do not
insert the logo over a background color except in instances
where this is necessary; for those times, the proper version of
the logo is shown here.



There may be a reason to put the logo on a black, Outsell green,
or Outsell gray background. Please use these variations only
after consulting the visual designer.




ON BLACK


Use the grayscale or black-and-white Outsell logo only when
absolutely necessary, i.e., in a newspaper ad that prints in black
and white only, or other similar instance. First preference is to
use the logo in grayscale before choosing the all-black version.
