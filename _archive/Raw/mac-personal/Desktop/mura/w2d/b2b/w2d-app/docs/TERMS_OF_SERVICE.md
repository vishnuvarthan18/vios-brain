## 1. What Wedding2day is

Wedding2day ("W2D", "the app", "we") is a business-to-business platform for the wedding trade in Tamil Nadu. It is open to businesses across all 29 wedding-trade categories we list in the app — decoration, catering, photography and videos, DJ, bridal makeup, mahal or mandabam, invitation, audio and lighting, and the rest.

The app has two parts:

- A trade section, where you can post items to sell, rent out, or resell, and post requirements for things you need. Other businesses respond to you directly.
- A public profile page, which you can create and share with anyone — including customers who do not use the app. See section 5.

Every business on W2D has a role: Vendor or Manufacturer. Vendors provide a service on the wedding day itself — photography, catering, DJ, bridal makeup, priest, musicians, venue, and so on — and they need materials to do it. Manufacturers make or supply those materials and products. Vendors ask; Manufacturers supply. Your role follows from the category you choose, and it decides what you can post. Section 3 explains how it is set and section 4 what it allows.

W2D is a connection service only. We do not buy, sell, own, inspect, deliver, or take payment for anything listed on the app. Every transaction — price, delivery, payment and terms — is agreed and carried out directly between users, off the platform, usually over phone or WhatsApp.


## 2. Who can use W2D

You must be at least 18 years old and running a genuine business or trade activity in the wedding industry. By creating an account you confirm that the details you give us are accurate and that they describe a real business.


## 3. Your account

- Signing up needs a working mobile number, verified by a one-time password. Keep access to that number and to your phone secure — anyone who can receive your OTP can reach your account.
- You choose one business category from our fixed list and one district. These are shown on everything you post, so other businesses know who they are dealing with. You can change them at any time in Profile → Edit Business Details.
- Your role — Vendor or Manufacturer — comes from the category you choose. You do not pick it separately, but we always show you which role your category gives you and ask you to confirm it before you continue, because it decides what you can post. A few categories could reasonably sit on either side; for those we set the more common one, and we say so on screen. If it is wrong for your business, contact us and we will change it.
- Changing your category can change your role, and therefore change what you can post. We tell you on the Edit Business Details screen before you save.
- A business has exactly one role. There is no dual registration, and we do not offer a way to hold both.
- You can change your registered mobile number in Profile → Change Phone Number. You verify the new number with an OTP and keep the same account, posts and history. Changing your number is not the same as deleting your account.

- One account per business. Do not create accounts to impersonate another business or to get around a suspension.

## 4. Posting on W2D — what your role allows

What you can post depends on your role, and the rule works in both directions:

- Manufacturers can post items to sell (new or used) and items to rent out, and can keep a catalog of their standing product range. Manufacturers cannot post requirements.
- Vendors can post requirements — "I need this" — which Manufacturers then answer. Vendors cannot post items for sale or rent, and do not have a catalog. Your public profile page (section 5) is where you show your work instead.

This is a deliberate limit, not a bug. The app exists to connect the two sides, so each side posts on its own side. We enforce it on our servers, not just in the app, so a post outside your role will not go through however it is attempted.

When you post an item, a requirement, or a catalog entry, you confirm that:

- The details, price, quantity and condition you describe are accurate.
- The photos are genuinely of the item and you have the right to use them.
- You are not posting prohibited, illegal, counterfeit, stolen or unsafe items.

You keep ownership of everything you upload. By posting it, you give us permission to display it inside the app, and — for anything you put on your public profile — on that public page, so the app can do the job you signed up for. That permission ends when you delete the content or your account, except where we are required to keep something by law.

New posts are reviewed before they appear. We may remove any post, or suspend any account, that breaks these terms or that other users report.


## 5. Your public profile page — please read this

You can create a public profile page for your business. This is optional and you choose whether to make one.

If you create one, the following becomes visible to ANYONE who has the link, with no login and no W2D account needed:

- Your business name
- Your business category and district
- Your role — whether you are a Vendor or a Manufacturer — so someone reading the page knows whether you provide a service or supply materials
- Any portfolio photos you add to it
- Your phone number, and your WhatsApp number if you add a different one — shown directly on the page, not hidden behind a tap

This is deliberate. The page exists so you can share it with customers and leads outside the app, and it cannot do that job if your contact details are hidden. It works differently from the trade section, where a phone number is only shown after another business taps to see it.

Two things to be aware of. First: anyone you share the link with can share it onward, and we cannot control that. Second: your page is not listed in any directory and cannot be searched or browsed — it is only reachable through the link itself — but it is public, so treat the link as public too.

Your catalog and your prices are never part of the public page. Those stay inside the app, visible only to signed-in businesses.

You can edit your public profile at any time in Profile → Public Profile, and deleting your account removes the page entirely.


## 6. Connecting with other businesses

In the trade section, tapping "I'm Interested" on a post shows you that business's phone number so you can contact them. On a requirement post it works the other way round: tapping "I Can Supply This" sends YOUR contact details to the Vendor who posted the requirement. Only Manufacturers can answer a requirement, and only Vendors can post one.

Revealing a number is per business, not per post. Once you have been shown a business's number you keep it, and looking at another of their posts costs you nothing more. If someone answers your requirement, you choose when to see each responder's number — their details are not handed over in bulk.

Contact numbers are not stored where other users can read them. Yours is only ever shown to a business that has revealed it through the app, or to anyone at all if you choose to put it on your public profile page (section 5). From the point a number is revealed:

- All negotiation, payment, delivery and dispute resolution happens directly between you and the other business, outside the app.
- W2D is not a party to any transaction and does not guarantee the identity, reliability or conduct of any user, or the outcome of any deal.
- We strongly recommend checking who you are dealing with and agreeing clear terms — price, delivery, payment — before you commit.

There is a daily limit on how many phone numbers one account can reveal, and it applies to every way of getting one — a post, a catalog page, or a response to your own requirement. It exists to stop bulk collection of other people's contact details. Do not scrape, copy or resell contact information from the app, and do not use it to send unsolicited marketing. We can see how many numbers an account has revealed, and we suspend accounts that look like they are collecting rather than trading.


## 7. Rentals

A post marked "For Rent" is a listing only. W2D does not handle bookings, dates, availability, deposits or returns. Everything about a rental is agreed directly between the two businesses.

## 11. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Delete Account. We will send a verification code to your registered number first, because deletion cannot be undone.

Deleting your account removes:

- Your profile — name, business name, category, role, district and phone number
- Every post you have made, and your catalog items
- Your public profile page, so the link stops working and your contact details are no longer published
- The photos you uploaded

Three things are kept, and none of them contains your personal details:

- Records that you showed interest in another business's post. These keep that business's own counts correct, and hold only an account reference that no longer points to anything.
- Records of which businesses were shown your number, and whose numbers you were shown. These hold two account references and a date, nothing more, and once your account is gone they point to nothing. They exist so the daily reveal limit cannot be reset by deleting and recreating an account.
- Any reports you filed, which our team needs for moderation.

If you cannot receive the verification code — for example you no longer have that number — write to us at the address in section 14 and we will delete the account for you.
