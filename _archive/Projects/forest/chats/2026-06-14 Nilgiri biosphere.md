Sathyamangalam Tiger Reserve (STR) — the standout records and facts:

- **Biggest tiger landscape in the world.** STR connects with Mudumalai, Bandipur, and BR Hills reserves to form the Nilgiri Biosphere landscape, which together holds the biggest tiger population in the world, at over 280 tigers.
- **A remarkable population recovery.** In 2009, camera trap surveys estimated only 10 tigers in the core; this rose to ~25 by 2011 and 87 by 2018, with recent counts around 88+.
- **TX2 Award winner.** In 2022, the reserve won the TX2 award by World Wide Fund in collaboration with wildlife conservation agencies for its growth in tiger population — an international honour for doubling tigers since 2010.
- **Unique geography.** It sits at the junction of the Eastern and Western Ghats, making it a rare ecological and genetic corridor linking multiple protected areas — terrain ranges 750m to 1,649m.
- **Size and status.** Covers ~1,411 km² across Talavady, Sathyamangalam, and Gobichettipalayam taluks of Erode district; declared a tiger reserve in 2013 (the fourth in Tamil Nadu under Project Tiger).

One open issue worth knowing, given your community focus: Tamil Nadu has a dismal record in distributing individual and community forest rights to tribal claimants in the STR area, leaving forest-dwelling communities awaiting rights under the Forest Rights Act even as tiger numbers are celebrated.
