## Recommendation: Lead on Facebook, repurpose to Instagram

For this exact content — local, parent-targeted, kids' education in a Tamil Nadu town — **Facebook is your primary platform.** Instagram is secondary.

**Why Facebook wins for you:**
- Your buyers (parents 30–45 in Erode) skew heavily toward Facebook over Instagram, and Facebook has broader audience reach especially among working professionals and parents, is more effective for regional/local marketing, and has high potential for education courses and local services.
- Meta Ads let you reach parents within a specific radius with demographic targeting — critical since your offer is geo-locked to Mettukadai, Erode. A boosted post or lead ad targeting parents of 8–15 year-olds within ~15km of Erode will outperform any organic post.
- Facebook lets you target parents of children in a specific age band using parental behaviour data and interests like education and child development — directly matching your "Ages 10–15."

**Why Instagram is secondary, not skippable:**
- Parents may not visit your campus immediately, but they will visit your Instagram — your social presence is now the first impression and builds emotional trust. Parents who see the FB ad will check your IG before enquiring.

## What to actually do

1. **Post the poster on both**, but put your ad budget behind the **Facebook** post (boost / lead ad).
2. **Format for each:** This poster is 5:7 portrait — fine for IG feed and FB feed. For IG, also crop a 9:16 version for Stories.
3. **The poster alone won't max reach.** Static images underperform video on both platforms.
