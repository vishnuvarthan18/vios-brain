# Change in plan

## Summary
**Conversation Overview**

This conversation continued an ongoing multi-session Meghalaya trip planning effort. The person is planning a 3-day trip (Saturday Sep 12 – Monday Sep 14) departing from Guwahati airport on a 8:05pm flight Monday. The locked itinerary had been previously established as "Plan A Rev 8," with Saturday covering Umiam Lake, Elephant Falls, Smit Village, Mawphlang Sacred Forest, Laitlum Canyons, and Police Bazar (overnight Shillong Zostel), Sunday covering Nongriat trek plus Sohra-area waterfalls (overnight Sohra/Bynardi homestay), and Monday covering the Sohra circuit, Mawlynnong, and the airport run.

The conversation's central issue was a discovered Sunday closure at Nongriat (Double Decker Root Bridge), confirmed through web searches and traveler accounts, which invalidated the locked Sunday placement of the trek. This triggered a full itinerary restructure debate. The person also wanted to add Pung Lyer Falls (Laitryngew cluster) to the plan, which had been previously rejected for geographic incompatibility. Claude repeatedly explained that Pung Lyer sits on the Shillong–Sohra road via Laitryngew, not on any existing route leg, requiring a dedicated detour. After extended back-and-forth, a working structure emerged: Saturday for Nongriat (landing Guwahati, driving to Tyrna, trekking, overnight Sohra), Sunday for the Sohra circuit plus Laitryngew cluster en route to Shillong (replacing Khoh Ramhah/Thangkharang with Mawphlang Sacred Forest per the person's preference), and Monday for Umiam Lake, Elephant Falls, Mawlynnong, and the airport.

A deep feasibility analysis then identified a critical failure in the Saturday plan: real Guwahati–Tyrna transit time is 5–5.5 hours (not the 2–2.5 hours previously assumed), meaning the trek would start around 1:30–2pm at best, conflicting with commonly cited 2pm last-entry cutoffs and creating a genuine safety risk descending 3,000+ stone steps after dark. The person proposed overnighting at Nongriat to eliminate the dusk-climb risk, specifically suggesting arriving Saturday and staying through Sunday morning. Claude flagged that the descent to Nongriat itself in the dark (if transit runs late) remains dangerous even with an overnight stay, and identified the hard cutoff decision point as: if not at Tyrna by 2–2:30pm, don't start the descent that day. The conversation ended with this risk unresolved and the plan not yet locked. Throughout, the person expressed strong preference for short, direct answers and pushed back explicitly when responses were too long or complex, preferring tables over prose for itinerary presentation.
