# Waiting list ticket refund policy

## Summary
**Conversation Overview**

The person sought help understanding the refund process for an Indian Railways (IRCTC) e-ticket that was booked online and remained on the waiting list (PQWL/2) after chart preparation. The conversation centered on two core questions: whether a refund would be issued automatically and whether a no-show by a confirmed passenger could result in seat allocation to the waitlisted passenger.

Claude explained that fully-waitlisted IRCTC e-tickets are automatically cancelled after chart preparation with a refund returned to the original payment source, and that no-show seats are not allotted to auto-cancelled e-ticket holders — only the onboard TTE can allocate vacant berths, prioritizing RAC and counter-waitlisted passengers. The person asked Claude to verify confidence levels and then requested confirmation against current IRCTC policy, prompting a web search. The search confirmed the auto-cancellation and auto-refund policy, with the only uncertainty being whether a small clerkage charge would be deducted. Claude advised the person to verify the final refund status and credited amount directly in their IRCTC account under Booked Ticket History using the relevant PNR, and noted that if travel on the intended date was still needed, booking a current or tatkal ticket on an alternative train would be the reliable path forward.
