# Sales

## Diagnostic (2026-09-01)
Level: **Absolute beginner** — no sales experience: no pitching, negotiating, closing, or cold outreach.

## Goal
General sales mastery — the universal skill (persuasion, negotiation, closing), not tied to one context (not just SaaS, not just pitching investors). Runs in parallel with every other domain, always — not sequenced by priority like the engineering subjects.

## Time budget
Continuous, small allocation alongside whichever engineering subject is active. Not a "phase" in the weekly time-split — think of it as always-on, lighter-touch practice (e.g. 30–60 min/week) rather than a dedicated block.

## Roadmap

### Phase 1 — Foundations of persuasion
- How people actually decide (emotion first, logic to justify) — not manipulation, understanding
- Listening over talking: most sales failure is talking too much, not too little
- Framing and positioning: same offer, different framing, different result

### Phase 2 — The sales conversation
- Discovery: asking questions that surface real needs, not pitching before understanding the problem
- Handling objections without getting defensive
- Closing: recognizing the moment, asking directly, without being pushy

### Phase 3 — Negotiation
- Anchoring, concessions, and knowing your walk-away point (BATNA)
- Win-win framing vs. zero-sum thinking
- Reading the other side's real constraints, not just their stated position

### Phase 4 — Applied practice
- Practice on real, low-stakes situations first (negotiating a price, pitching an idea to a friend)
- Since this runs parallel to engineering learning: practice explaining/selling your own projects as they develop (a natural, low-pressure training ground)
- Eventually: real pitches — to users, collaborators, or investors, as opportunities arise
