A critical operational rule was established: every session must produce 5 photos and 1 short video, as AI-generated content without real student imagery produces hollow filler. The person's team are beginners who generate content using AI, so all strategy and materials were designed for that capability level with a 10-minute weekly founder approval step. Broad generic tags (#STEMEducation, #Robotics, #AI) were flagged as ineffective and excluded.

The person confirmed the school location details (2 Coimbatore, 1 Erode, 1 Salem) are for hashtag targeting only and must never appear in panel copy. Throughout, the person requested a plain, warm South Indian English tone with no AI filler words, proof over hype, and short concrete sentences — and asked for reasoning behind every change so their beginner team can learn the pattern rather than just copy outputs.
