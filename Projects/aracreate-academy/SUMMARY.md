# PROJECT: araCreate Academy

## 1. What this project is
- **Goal:** Help run and grow araCreate Academy, araCreate's kids' AI, robotics and STEM programme. Vishnu helps / works on it (content, decks, social, VCET bootcamp); he is not the founder (confirmed 2026-10-06).
- **Who it is for / client:** Students in grades 5 to 9 (poster says ages 10–15), their parents, and school principals. Mostly government and government-aided schools in the Kongu region (Coimbatore, Erode, Salem). Centre in Mettukadai, Erode.
- **Why it exists:** Schools cannot teach deep robotics, AI and electronics with normal teachers. araCreate sends its own engineering trainers for weekly in-school sessions.

## 2. Status now (as of 2026-09-19)
- Programme runs weekly in-school sessions: STEM basics, electronics, embedded systems, sensors, IoT, robotics, AI and 3D.
- Scale (June 2026): 4 schools (2 Coimbatore, 1 Erode, 1 Salem), 700+ students. Do not put school details in public posts.
- Model: about ₹5,000 per school per month (trainers, curriculum, basic materials).
- Inside the School Innovation Development Project (SIDP): 7+ student projects selected at state level.
- Social media plan done: Instagram (parents) + LinkedIn (principals), five content pillars, 10-panel carousel and first 10 posts written; Word brief for the design team made.
- Electronics trainer job post (freelance) written (2026-07-16).
- 15-day training proposal deck (Model A Product Engineering Bootcamp, Model B Electronics Launchpad) finished as two separate PDFs; client gave positive feedback.
- Day 1–2 quiz (14 questions, pipe format) made on 2026-09-19, so a training batch seems to be running (not sure which).
- Not done: real page handles for paid promotion (no verified list); mobile version of the HTML deck was dropped.
