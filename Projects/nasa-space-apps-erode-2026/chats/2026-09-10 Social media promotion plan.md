## 1. Answers to your two questions

**Can we use our own registration form?**

- Yes. Nothing in the NASA Local Lead Terms bans it.
- But NASA platform registration is still compulsory. Our form is extra, not a replacement.
- Rule: no fee. "Local Leads may not charge participants."
- So our form must capture their **NASA username** — that is how we verify they are really registered.
- Safe wording on our form: "Step 1 – register on spaceappschallenge.org and pick Erode. Step 2 – fill this form."

**Branding limits (affects every post)**

- We may call ourselves "NASA Space Apps Challenge Local Lead of Erode".
- We may edit **only the text** in the official Local Event logo template.
- We may NOT use the NASA insignia (the round logo) without written permission.
- We may NOT imply NASA endorses araCreate, VCET, or any sponsor.
- By taking part, event photos/video can be used by NASA.
