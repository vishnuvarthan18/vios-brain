## 4. Physical facts a designer needs (from fetched pages)

- Leaf is talipot or palmyra, dried flat in sun, cut with a curved knife to long narrow strips, edges trimmed. Source: [Ancient Madurai](https://ancientmadurai.com/what-is-olai-chuvadi-palm-leaf-manuscript/).
- Treated with turmeric and lime, then neem oil. This gives the amber to brown leaf colour.
- One or two holes near the ends, a cord through them, and wooden boards on top and bottom. Kept in wooden boxes.
- Written with an iron or wood/bamboo stylus (ezhuthani, also narayam). Scratches are then rubbed with soot or dark paste, so the script shows dark on a light amber field. Old leaves show browned edges.
- Round Tamil letter shapes came from the medium: straight lines split the leaf ([Ola leaf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ola_leaf)).- Sources on stylus: [Narayam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narayam), [Tamil Treasure](http://tamil-treasure.blogspot.com/2011/09/stylus-ezuththaani.html), [Michael Backman stylus](https://www.michaelbackmanltd.com/object/palm-leaf-scribes-iron-silver-inlaid-stylus-trimming-knife-sri-lanka-19th-century/) (Sri Lankan, photo not reusable).
- Design pin boards: [Behance search](https://www.behance.net/search/projects/palm%20leaf%20manuscript), [Dribbble palm leaf](https://dribbble.com/tags/palm_leaf). Inspiration only.
