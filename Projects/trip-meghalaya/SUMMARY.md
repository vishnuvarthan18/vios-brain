# PROJECT: Meghalaya Trip (Sep 2026)

## 1. What this project is
- **Goal:** A short adventure trip to Meghalaya for 3 people, with as many "untouched" places, treks and waterfalls as possible.
- **Who it is for / client:** Vishnu and 2 friends (personal trip).
- **Why it exists:** Vishnu wanted a 4-day trip to the North or Northeast that covers a Saturday and Sunday, on a small budget.

## 2. Status now (as of 2026-09-13)
- Trip happened: 12-14 Sep 2026 (Google export said "about 11-13 Sep"; the day-by-day chats and the 12 Sep flight support 12-14). Erode → Bangalore by car, flight BLR → Guwahati, self-drive rental car in Meghalaya, flight back on Monday evening.
- Day 1 (Sat 12 Sep) done: landed Guwahati, drove to Sohra, saw Wei Sawdong Falls and Nohkalikai Falls. Skipped Nongriat. Stayed at Da-Da Tourist Cottage & Campsite, Sohra.
- Day 2 (Sun 13 Sep) done: Sohra stops (Seven Sisters Falls, Mawsmai Cave, Lyngksiar Falls, Prut Falls, Thangkharang/Kynrem area) then Shillong for the night; Police Bazar.
- Day 3 (Mon 14 Sep) plan: Mawphlang Sacred Forest, Smit Village, Laitlum Canyons, Umiam Lake, then Guwahati airport. Not confirmed in chat if all were done.
- Dropped: Nongriat Double Decker root bridge, Mawlynnong, Dawki, Elephant Falls, Arwah Cave, Pung Lyer, Kyntaiai Valley.

## 3. Next steps
1. None. Trip is over.
2. Ideas saved for a future trip: Kyntaiai Valley (best Oct-Nov), Nongriat root bridge (closed Sundays), Wari Chora (needs more days).
