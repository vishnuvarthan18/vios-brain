**What this day costs you:**

-   Roughly 8 hours in the car for one attraction.

-   The entire Sohra circuit --- Nohkalikai, Seven Sisters, Mawsmai
    Cave, Khoh Ramhah, Thangkharang, and the new Laitryngew cluster.
    Sohra becomes a bed and nothing else, and Monday cannot absorb
    them.

-   Season risk: the trek sources rate June--September as unsuitable ---
    muddy and slippery, with October--November as the proper window.

-   Vehicle risk: a Dzire is low-clearance. Zola must be told the route
    includes the Mawkyrwat/Mawsaw interior before pickup day, not on it.

**Verdict: not worth it on this trip. Kyntaiai deserves a November visit
with a night in Mawkyrwat, not a 19-hour bracket around a 3-hour walk.**

Confirmed

-   Kyntaiai Valley --- real. At Mawsaw, South West Khasi Hills. Sources
    place it 60--100 km from Shillong at 2--4 hrs; trailhead trek 2--3
    km each way, 60--90 min per leg, moderate, poorly marked, local
    guide strongly recommended.

-   Mawkyrwat --- 78 km from Shillong per the district administration.
    Sumos, tourist taxis and buses run from Iew-Duh (Bara Bazaar),
    Shillong.

-   No direct Mawkyrwat--Sohra road worth planning around. The practical
    return is back through Shillong, which is why the original 3--4 hr
    estimate for that leg was optimistic by roughly two hours.

-   Shillong--Dawki road --- the closure was a major landslide on 23 Jun
    2026; your Aug 10 reopening stands. Re-check ~5 days out
    regardless.

-   Kaziranga and Manas --- closed for monsoon on these dates. Correctly
    excluded.

5. Weather --- unusually favourable

Meghalaya's 2026 monsoon is running far below normal: 784.8 mm against a
normal 1,838.1 mm through 12 August, a 57% deficit. IMD's extended-range
forecast called both 14--20 and 21--27 August deficient across the
state.

**What that means for you:**

-   Materially lower landslide and road-washout risk than a typical
    mid-September.

-   Trails and cave approaches drier and safer.

-   The waterfalls will be weaker than the postcard versions. Nohkalikai
    and Seven Sisters depend on volume --- and so will Wah Umkrem.

-   Do not treat this as settled. Re-pull the IMD Shillong
    extended-range forecast around 5 September, when it first covers
    your dates.
