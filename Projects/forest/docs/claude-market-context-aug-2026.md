## 1. The AI/geospatial skill set is dual-use — this is the safety net

- **64% of all new GCC roles in India require AI, data science or intelligent automation skills.** AI/data is the fastest-growing GCC function at **+38% YoY**.
- AI/ML engineers command a **40–60% pay premium** over equivalent-experience backend engineers.
- GCC salary band, AI/ML engineer, 3–5 years' experience: **₹26–45 LPA** (Bengaluru baseline; Hyderabad/Pune ~10–15% lower).
- Current income for comparison: ₹40,000/month = ₹4.8 LPA.

**What this means for the forest plan:** the Track A skill stack (Python, rasters, GeoPandas, Earth Engine, computer vision, edge AI, pipeline work) is the same stack that sells into a GCC. If conservation tech does not produce a living within 24 months, there is a well-paid fallback that requires no retraining. **Track A is not a career gamble against a tech career — it is the same skills pointed at a different employer.**

## 2. Domain-specific AI is the defensible version — which validates going deeper, not broader

The career map's own caveat: "Become an AI engineer" is advice a million people are receiving simultaneously, and generic AI skills will commoditise the way generic full-stack skills did. The defensible version is **AI applied to a domain you understand**.

**Implication:** the Senna mapping project, camera-trap ML, bioacoustics work and Western Ghats-specific geospatial output are not a detour from employability. They are the moat. Time spent on hyper-local, place-based forest data work beats time spent on generic ML certifications on both tracks simultaneously.

Related durable-skill list from the same brief (things that hold value as code gets cheap): judgment under ambiguity, domain depth, verification, systems thinking, trust and communication. Four of the five are built by staying somewhere long enough to understand it deeply.

## 3. The rupee at ₹95/$ is a real tailwind on international remote work

- **Rupee/USD: ₹95.24**, near record low; breached ₹96 in Q1 2026.
- India CPI (Jun 2026): **4.38%**, rising; RBI's own forecast path reaches **5.9% by Q3 FY27**.
- Brent crude **$89.77, +36.8% YoY** — the root cause of most of the above.

**Implication:** a $1,200/month remote conservation-tech contract is ~₹1.14 lakh/month at today's rate. That is roughly a 14% currency tailwind versus two years ago, on exactly the income stream `no-government-track-plan.md` identifies as the money track.

**This reorders Track A priorities:** international remote conservation-tech work (WILDLABS network, foreign-funded orgs, USD/GBP grants) is worth more per hour of effort than domestic forest-department GIS contracts at ₹35,000/month. Pursue both, but weight the international side higher than the plan currently does.

Caveat: currency tailwinds reverse. A Hormuz settlement would strengthen the rupee and shrink this advantage. Do not build a plan that only works at ₹95.

## 4. Correction to `no-government-track-plan.md` — the runway assumption is optimistic

The existing plan treats the current ₹40,000/month tech income as stable runway through to 2028.

The macro brief says this is less safe than assumed:

- TCS announced **12,000+** cuts (~2% of headcount); running total through 2026 is above **23,000**. Across the top Indian IT firms, reductions run into the tens of thousands.
- Indian tech revenue grew **6.1% to $315B in FY26** while headcount grew only **2.3% to 5.95 million**. Revenue and headcount have decoupled. The industry is not shrinking — it is growing output while holding headcount flat.
- The roles shrinking fastest: manual QA, L1/L2 support, routine app maintenance, generalist full-stack CRUD work. Small-studio and client-delivery work sits close to this tier.

**Action implied:** build the six-month expense buffer faster than the plan schedules. The exit from tech may not stay entirely voluntary, and a forced exit with two months of savings is a different situation from a chosen exit with six.

**Not a reason to panic.** The aggregate Indian economy is fine — 86% of SBI's 50 leading indicators accelerated in Q1 FY27, bank credit +17.7%, passenger vehicle sales +24%. The pain is sector-specific to IT services, not economy-wide. Treat the sector signal and the macro signal as separate.

## Figures worth re-checking before acting on them

- Salary bands come partly from recruitment-industry sources with an incentive toward optimism. Treat LPA ranges as negotiating anchors, not guarantees.
- Macro data revises. Oil, rupee and CPI figures above are 12 Aug 2026 snapshots and the oil price drives nearly all of them.
- The 10-year role forecasts in the career map are reasoning from trend, not evidence. The 2–3 year read is solid; the decade view is not.
