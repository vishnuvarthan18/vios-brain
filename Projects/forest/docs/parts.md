1. Fix their code and they will notice you

There is a small group in Goa called Technology for Wildlife. They fly drones, use satellite data, and make maps to help protect wildlife. 
GitHub

They put their computer programs on a free public website called GitHub. Anyone can open it and read the code. They have tools there for planning drone flight paths in QGIS. 
Techforwildlife

Here is the point. If you find a mistake in their code and fix it, you send the fix back to them. It is called a pull request. Their team reads it. They see your name and your work.

No form. No interview. Nobody asks for your degree.

Think of it like this. Everyone else is standing in a queue outside their office. You are walking in and helping with the work already on the table.

Start at github.com/techforwildlife.

2. A website where these people gather

It is called WILDLABS. It is a group of about 6,000 people in 120 countries who all work on technology for wildlife. They talk about camera traps, machine learning, and sensors. They also post jobs there. 
Wildlabs

Almost nobody from India uses it. If you write even a few good posts there, people will know your name.

3. Get money to do your own project

You do not always need someone to hire you. You can ask for a grant. A grant is money given to you to do a project.

India is on the eligible list for Rufford Small Grants. The first grant goes up to £7,000, which is roughly 7 lakh rupees, and there is no deadline. You can apply any month. 
Ngoscope
Ruffordsmallgrants

Two problems to solve first. They prefer people doing or just finished a Master's. And they do not pay the money to a person. Only to an organisation. So you need a college or an NGO to hold the money for you.

4. Write to a person, not to an office

Do not send applications to "HR". Find the actual scientist.

Example: Ganesh Raghunathan works at Nature Conservation Foundation. He flies drones to watch elephants in Valparai, Tamil Nadu, along with the local forest department staff. 
Techforwildlife

Valparai is about three hours from your house. Write to him. Tell him one specific thing you can build for him.

One good email beats fifty applications.

5. The video nobody has time to watch

Tamil Nadu forest teams already fly drones in 13 ranges. Drones record many hours of video. Somebody has to watch all of it to spot animals. Nobody has that much time.

A computer program can watch it instead. That is your skill, and nobody has taken this job yet.
