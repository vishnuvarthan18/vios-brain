## Positioning used

Every message made four moves, in this order:
1. Here is what I built (sathyamangalam.online, 1,893 species/taxa, sourced from management plans, gazettes, published research)
2. This is **not an organisation** — one person's independent research, no government affiliation
3. Why: Sathyamangalam is under-documented; attention goes to Masinagudi/Mudumalai
4. The ask

**Email ask:** opened with a specific unresolved-figure question (highest reply-rate lever), then data / collaboration / general support. Funding never named directly — carried by "support for independent work like this."

**WhatsApp ask:** collaboration-first, not corrections. Shorter, link-forward.
