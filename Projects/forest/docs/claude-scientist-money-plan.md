# Paying For The Degree: Your Money Plan

**Your situation:** Job earning ₹40,000/month. EMI of ₹20,000/month with 3 to 5 years left. Nobody funding you but you.

---

## The short answer

**WII Dehradun is out.** It costs ₹6,50,000. You cannot pay that and you should not take a second loan for it. Forget it. (If you happen to win one of their 8 free scholarships later, great. But do not build a plan on it.)

**NCBS Bangalore is your plan.** They give every selected student a studentship that covers **academic fees and living expenses**, and hostel accommodation. So the degree itself costs you nothing.

**The only real problem is your EMI.** For the 2 years you are studying, you cannot earn ₹40,000. But you still owe ₹20,000 every month.

So the whole question is: **how do you find ₹20,000 × 24 months = ₹4,80,000?**

---

## The good news about timing

NCBS runs only every 2 years. The next batch starts **August 2028**. Applications open around **September 2027**.

That feels like a long wait. It is actually a gift. It gives you **24 months to save money** while still earning ₹40,000.

---

## How to find the ₹4.8 lakh

### Step 1 — Save ₹15,000 every month, starting this month

₹40,000 income minus ₹20,000 EMI leaves ₹20,000. Live on ₹5,000 and save ₹15,000.

**24 months × ₹15,000 = ₹3,60,000**

That is most of the way there. Put it in a separate account you do not touch. Not the account your salary lands in.

### Step 2 — Go to your bank and ask to extend the loan tenure

This is the single easiest win and most people never try it.

If you stretch a loan over more years, the monthly EMI drops. Going from ₹20,000 to around ₹13,000 is realistic on many loans. You pay more total interest, but your monthly burden falls by ₹7,000.

If your EMI becomes ₹13,000:

- You need only **₹13,000 × 24 = ₹3,12,000** for the study years
- **Your ₹3,60,000 savings already covers it, with money left over**

Do this in the last few months before you leave the job, not now. Right now the higher EMI is fine because you are earning.

### Step 3 — Do small freelance work during the degree

You are a CS person. Even ₹5,000 to ₹8,000 a month from small remote jobs closes any remaining gap. Be realistic though: the NCBS course is field-heavy, you will be in forests, and you will not have much time. Treat this as a bonus, not a plan.

### Together

| Source | Amount |
|---|---|
| Savings over 24 months | ₹3,60,000 |
| EMI reduced by extending tenure | Need drops to ₹3,12,000 |
| **Result** | **Covered, with a small cushion** |

## The cheap backup plan — start this now

**IGNOU M.Sc. Environmental Science**

- **₹17,000 total** for the whole degree
- 2 years, distance learning
- You keep your ₹40,000 job the entire time
- Eligibility says "Graduation in Science." Your B.Sc. Computer Science is a science degree, but **call IGNOU and confirm before paying**

**Why do this:**

The ICFRE Scientist-B job asks for a "First Class Master's degree." It does not say the degree must be full-time or residential. An IGNOU degree is UGC recognised and valid for government jobs.

So for ₹17,000 and no loss of income, you get a Master's in hand.

**Be honest about its limit:** a distance degree with no fieldwork will not make you competitive against NCBS graduates for research jobs. Selection panels can tell. It is a paper qualification, not a training.

But it costs almost nothing, it does not stop you doing anything else, and if NCBS never works out you are not left with zero. Start it in the January 2027 cycle.

## Three rules

1. **Do not take a second loan for education.** You already have one. Adding another while your income goes to zero is how this plan breaks.
2. **The savings account is untouchable.** Separate bank, no card. If you spend it in 2027, there is no 2028.
3. **If you get promoted or your income rises, save the difference.** Do not raise your spending. Every extra ₹5,000/month saved is ₹1,20,000 more runway.
