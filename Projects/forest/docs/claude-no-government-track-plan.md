# The Revised Plan: No Government Job

**For:** Vishnuvarthan Venkatapathy, Erode
**Date:** 12 August 2026
**Supersedes:** `six-track-master-plan.md`, `government-scientist-master-plan.md`, `scientist-degree-path.md`

## What changed

You decided against a permanent government job. Not the pay, not the difficulty — the closed circle. Uniform, hierarchy, transfer orders, thirty years in one system.

**Contract and project work with government-funded institutes stays in play.** WII project posts, forest department GIS contracts, ICFRE project associate roles are 6-month to 3-year engagements. You sign, you work, you leave. No lock-in, no pension, no hierarchy owning you. That is a different thing from a permanent post and you kept it.

**Three things you no longer have to do, starting today:**

1. **The 25 km walk in 4 hours.** That standard existed only for the Forester physical test. Gone.
2. **The height and chest measurement.** That gate no longer decides anything. Stop worrying about 163 cm.
3. **The "First Class printed on the marksheet" question.** That was an ICFRE-specific rejection rule. No non-government employer checks this. Gate 2 in the old plan is void.

That is a meaningful amount of anxiety deleted in one decision.

## What remains: three tracks

### Track A — Conservation tech (the money track)

**This is now your primary track, not your fallback.** It is the only remaining option that pays at or above your current ₹40,000, and it is the one your existing skills already reach.

| | |
|---|---|
| Roles | GIS/remote-sensing analyst, geospatial developer, drone/imagery pipeline work, camera-trap and bioacoustics ML |
| Employers | Forest dept GIS contracts (₹35,000 seen at Koraput), WII project posts (Project Associate ₹30,000–33,000 + HRA; Project Scientist-I ₹77,000 + HRA), private geospatial and climate-tech firms, international conservation-tech orgs hiring remote |
| Needs | Geoinformatics credential + a real public portfolio. **Not a Master's for most of it** |
| Timeline | Fastest thing on the list. Months, not years |

The international angle matters more now than it did. Conservation tech hires remote, pays in foreign currency, and does not care that your degree is Computer Science — it cares what is on your GitHub. WILDLABS is the centre of that world and it is free to join.

### Track B — NGO field research

Keystone Foundation (Kotagiri, 45 min away), NCF, ATREE, WCS India, CWS.

₹15,000–33,000. Real forest work, real people, no hierarchy trap. Blocked by the loan, not by qualifications.

**Start volunteering now.** Weekends cost you travel and nothing else, and the sector hires almost entirely on who knows you.

### Track C — Naturalist / lodge

₹12,000–20,000 plus free food and accommodation. You wake up inside a tiger reserve. No degree required at all.

This is the purest version of what you say you want. **It is blocked by exactly one thing: the ₹20,000 EMI.**

## The M.Sc. question — reconsidered

The M.Sc. Environmental Science existed in the old plan mainly to unlock ICFRE Scientist-B. That door is now closed by choice.

**Recommendation: still enrol, but demote it.** ₹17,000 at IGNOU while keeping your job is cheap, and it still unlocks WII project posts and senior NGO roles where a Master's is a hard filter. But it is no longer the spine of the plan, and none of its old anxieties apply.

**Promote the Geoinformatics PG Certificate instead (₹6,000, IGNOU).** That credential is what converts "Computer Science graduate" into "eligible for geospatial conservation posts" on subject lists where CS is not named. For Track A it does more work than the M.Sc. does.

If you had to pick one: **geoinformatics, not the M.Sc.**

## The loan is now the whole game

Look at what's left. Track A pays around what you earn. Tracks B and C pay half or less.

With a ₹20,000 EMI against ₹40,000 income, **you cannot take Track B or C at all.** They are not options; they are things you can read about. The loan is the only thing standing between you and a job you could start next month with no further qualification.

In the old plan the loan was Gate 5, listed last. **In this plan it is Gate 1.**

Every rupee that would have gone to exam coaching, application fees, and travel to test centres now goes here. Get the exact numbers from the bank this week: outstanding, rate, remaining tenure, prepayment penalty. Then build a kill schedule.

## Revised gates

**Gate 1 — Now: the loan.** Get real numbers. Build a payoff schedule. Everything else waits behind this.

**Gate 2 — December 2026: portfolio check.** Is the Senna project public, documented, and written up on WILDLABS? If yes, you are employable in Track A. If not, nothing else you did this year matters as much.

**Gate 3 — January 2027: enrol.** Geoinformatics PG Certificate (₹6,000) — non-negotiable. M.Sc. Environmental Science (₹17,000) — optional, take it if the money is comfortable.

**Gate 4 — Mid 2027: first contract.** Apply to every WII and IFGTB round (every 2–3 months) and every WILDLABS posting you half-qualify for. Target: one contract offer, even at lower pay, to break the "no conservation experience" loop.

**Gate 5 — EMI below ₹10,000: Tracks B and C unlock.** The naturalist and NGO paths become survivable. This is the moment the forest life you actually described becomes reachable.

## Rules that survive unchanged

1. **Never resign without a signed offer.** More true now, not less — you have removed the option with pension and job security.
2. **Apply to everything.** Contract rounds come every 2–3 months. Being registered when a window opens is the whole game.
3. **Review every six months.** Which track answered you? Weight that one.
4. **Keep the ₹40,000 job.** It funds the loan payoff, which funds your freedom.
