## The core idea

You are not choosing between six careers. You are building **one set of assets** that six different doors happen to accept.

| Asset you build | Which tracks it feeds |
|---|---|
| **Physical fitness** — 25 km walk capability | Forester, ACF, naturalist, all field work |

| **Loan cleared** | Naturalist, NGO field work, trek leading — everything low-paid |

Six assets. Six doors. **Every hour you spend feeds two or three tracks at once.**

## Decision gates

These are the moments where the plan branches. Do not guess — measure, then decide.

### Gate 1 — This week: measure your height

**163 cm or above?**

- **Yes** → Tracks 1 and 2 are live. Forester becomes your primary target, because it is the only one you already qualify for that gives you forest life *and* good money.
- **No, and not ST** → Tracks 1 and 2 close permanently. Put that energy into tracks 3, 4 and 5. Not a disaster — it just removes ambiguity.

This one measurement reshapes everything. Do it first.

### Gate 2 — By December 2026: the IGNOU "First Class" question

Email IGNOU and ask whether the M.Sc. Environmental Science degree or marksheet **prints the words "First Class."** ICFRE rejects degrees where it is not printed, regardless of percentage.

- **Yes** → Enrol January 2027. ₹17,000, keep your job.
- **No or unclear** → Enrol in a regular M.Sc. at Bharathiar (Coimbatore) or Periyar (Salem) instead. Costs more time; removes all risk.

### Gate 3 — August 2027: the 25 km walk

Attempt it properly and time yourself.

- **Under 4 hours** → Forester track is genuinely live. Shift study weight toward the exam syllabus.
- **Over** → Keep training another year, or accept that tracks 1 and 2 are not realistic and concentrate on 3, 4, 5.

### Gate 4 — December 2028: M.Sc. in hand

- Apply to every ICFRE, CPCB and TNPCB cycle from here on
- Also unlocks WII Project Associate at ₹30,000–33,000 + HRA, and Project Scientist-I at ₹77,000 + HRA
- You will be 26. Nine years of eligibility left

### Gate 5 — Ongoing: the loan

Once the EMI is cleared or reduced below ₹10,000, tracks 5 and 6 open up. Naturalist and NGO field work become survivable.

**Track this separately from everything else.** It is the quiet unlock for half the list.

## Rules

1. **Never resign without a signed offer letter.** With a ₹20,000 EMI, a gap between jobs ends this plan.
2. **Apply to everything, always.** An application costs a few hundred rupees and a weekend. Rejection costs nothing. The only real failure is not being registered when a window opens.
3. **Do not pick a favourite too early.** You have six years minimum. Let the results tell you which track is working.
4. **Review every six months.** Which track gave you the warmest response? Put more weight there. Which gave silence for a year? Reduce it.
5. **Keep the ₹40,000 job until something better is signed.** It funds every other part of this.
