# Jumping Now: The 90-Day Plan

**Date:** 11 August 2026
**Your position:** ₹40,000/month job. ₹20,000/month EMI with 3–5 years left. You do not want to wait until 2028.

---

## The honest picture

You can jump. But not into a degree — admissions for 2026 are all closed. You jump into a **job** instead.

Three things are in your way. All three are fixable inside 90 days.

---

## Wall 1 — Your degree subject is not on their lists

This is the one nobody told you.

WII just recruited 50 Technical Assistants for the All India Tiger Estimation project. Look at who they accepted:

> "B.Sc. (Wildlife / Zoology / Ecology / Environment / **Geoinformatics** / Life Sciences / Agriculture / Forestry)"

**Computer Science is not on that list. Geoinformatics is.**

This is why your applications would get rejected at screening, before any human reads them. Not because you cannot do the work. Because a clerk ticking boxes cannot find your subject on the list.

**The fix is cheap and fast.**

| Course | Time | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| IGNOU PG Certificate in Geoinformatics (PGCGI) | 6 months | about ₹6,000 |
| IGNOU PG Diploma in Geoinformatics (PGDGI) | 1 year | about ₹15,700 |

Any graduate is eligible. Distance mode. You keep your job. Next admission cycle is **January 2027**.

Once "Geoinformatics" is on your CV, you clear the screening filter for a whole category of jobs that reject you today.

Also free and immediately useful: **IIRS (ISRO) outreach courses** — online, no fee, and the most respected non-degree geospatial credential in India. Start one this month while you wait for the IGNOU cycle.

## Wall 2 — The money does not work yet

Entry-level conservation jobs pay:

| Post | Pay |
|---|---|
| BNHS Junior Project Fellow | ₹25,000 |
| WII Technical Assistant | ₹27,000 + HRA |
| WII Project Assistant | ₹27,000 + HRA |
| WII Project Associate-I | ₹30,000–37,000 + HRA |
| WII Senior Project Associate | ₹57,000 + HRA |

Do the maths on your life:

| | Today | If you jump at ₹27,000 |
|---|---|---|
| Income | ₹40,000 | ₹27,000 |
| EMI | ₹20,000 | ₹20,000 |
| **Left to live on** | **₹20,000** | **₹7,000** |

₹7,000 a month is not survivable, and you would be living away from home.

**The fix: go to your bank this month and extend the loan tenure.**

Stretching the loan over more years drops the monthly EMI. Going from ₹20,000 to around ₹13,000 is realistic. You pay more interest over the full life of the loan — that is the real cost — but it changes your monthly life completely:

| | With EMI at ₹13,000 |
|---|---|
| Income | ₹27,000 |
| EMI | ₹13,000 |
| **Left to live on** | **₹14,000** |

₹14,000 with hostel or shared accommodation in a field posting is tight but liveable.

**This one phone call is what makes jumping possible.** Everything else is secondary. Do it first.

## What you actually do, week by week

### Weeks 1–2 (now)

- **Call your bank.** Ask what the EMI becomes if the tenure is extended by 2 or 3 years. Get the exact number in writing.
- **Register at wiirp.com.** Upload documents. Be ready.
- **Start a free IIRS online course** in remote sensing or GIS.
- Do not resign. Nothing has changed yet.

### Weeks 3–8

- **Build one real project.** The Senna and Lantana mapping work using Google Earth Engine and Sentinel-2 imagery. Free data, free tools, no permission needed. Put it on GitHub with screenshots and a clear README. This is what you send instead of a covering letter.
- **Email Keystone Foundation** in Kotagiri (kf@keystone-foundation.org). Two hours from you. Ask to volunteer on weekends. Say what you can build, not what you want.
- **Email Technology for Wildlife** (contact@techforwildlife.com) after sending them one small pull request on their public GitHub. This is the least crowded door on the whole list.

### Weeks 9–12

- **Apply to the October–November WII round** the day it opens.
- **Extend the loan** once you have a real offer in hand, not before.
- **Enrol IGNOU Geoinformatics** in the January 2027 cycle.

### Rule

**Do not resign until you hold a signed offer letter.** With a ₹20,000 EMI and no savings, a gap between jobs is the thing that ends this plan.

## Where the degree fits now

It does not disappear. It moves behind the job.

Once you are inside — earning ₹27,000 at WII or an NGO — the degree gets easier, not harder:

- You will be in the field, which is what NCBS and WII interview panels actually care about
- Some project staff get institutional support to study
- WII's own M.Sc. raises the in-service age limit to **35** for Forest and Wildlife Department staff, versus 25 for outsiders
- The IGNOU M.Sc. Geoinformatics can be finished part-time while employed, and a first-class Master's is exactly what the ICFRE Scientist-B post asks for

**So the order flips.** Not: study for two years, then get a job. Instead: get the job now, then collect the degree while inside.

That is the version that works with a ₹20,000 EMI.
