**Your 30-day practice path:**

- **Days 1–5** — Single notes only. Pucker on hole 4, get clean blow and draw. Don't move until it sounds clean.
- **Days 6–14** — Move across holes 4–6. Play Mary Had a Little Lamb slowly with a metronome app.
- **Days 15–30** — Add holes 1–3 for draw notes. Start feeling the blues scale. Let rhythm come naturally.

**One rule above all:** slow and relaxed beats fast and tense every time. If a note sounds choked or squeaky, you're blowing too hard or gripping too tight.

**1. Tomlin Leckie — best overall for beginners**

→ Start with his **"Free 4-Week Blues Harmonica Foundation"** series on his channel.
→ YouTube: search **Tomlin Leckie harmonica**

**2. Adam Gussow — best for blues depth**
His channel features beginner to advanced instructional videos and is one of the most respected blues harmonica teaching resources on YouTube, with 191K subscribers. His explanations of *why* things work musically are unusually good.
→ YouTube: search **Gussow classic blues harmonica**

## Your exact learning order

1. **Week 1–2** — Tomlin Leckie's beginner YouTube videos, in order. Single notes only.
2. **Week 3–4** — His 12-bar blues beginner lesson. Start feeling rhythm.
3. **Month 2+** — Add Adam Gussow for blues expression and technique depth.

Don't jump between teachers in the first month. Tomlin's structure is enough — stick with it.
